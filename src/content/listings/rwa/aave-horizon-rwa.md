## Getting Started

1. **Connect a wallet** — Visit the Aave Horizon market and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask or a WalletConnect-supported wallet.
2. **Verify institutional eligibility** — Horizon is designed for institutional participants; ensure your entity meets any applicable access or KYC requirements for the market.
3. **Deposit tokenized RWAs as collateral** — Supply supported tokenized real-world assets into the market to use as collateral against which you can borrow.
4. **Borrow stablecoins** — Once collateral is posted, borrow supported stablecoins at variable interest rates, subject to the market's loan-to-value parameters.

## Key Features

- **RWA Collateral Support** — Accepts tokenized real-world assets as collateral, connecting off-chain value to on-chain liquidity.
- **Stablecoin Borrowing** — Institutions can unlock stablecoin liquidity without liquidating their tokenized asset positions.
- **Aave v3 Infrastructure** — Built on Aave's battle-tested, audited v3 protocol with established risk parameters and non-custodial design.
- **Ethereum-Native** — Operates fully on Ethereum, benefiting from its security, composability, and institutional-grade settlement finality.
- **Institutional Focus** — Tailored for institutional borrowers seeking compliant, transparent on-chain credit facilities backed by real-world assets.
- **Transparent Risk Parameters** — Collateral ratios, liquidation thresholds, and interest rates are publicly visible and governed on-chain.
