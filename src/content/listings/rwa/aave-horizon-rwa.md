## Getting Started

1. **Connect a wallet** — Navigate to the Aave Horizon market and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask or a WalletConnect-enabled wallet.
2. **Verify eligibility** — Horizon is designed for institutional participants; ensure your tokenized RWA collateral is supported within the market.
3. **Deposit collateral** — Supply your tokenized real-world assets as collateral into the Horizon lending pool.
4. **Borrow stablecoins** — Once collateral is posted, borrow supported stablecoins against your RWA position at variable interest rates.

## Key Features

- **RWA Collateral Support** — Accepts tokenized real-world assets such as treasury-backed tokens as collateral, connecting off-chain value to on-chain liquidity.
- **Stablecoin Borrowing** — Institutions can draw stablecoin liquidity against their RWA holdings without selling underlying positions.
- **Aave V3 Infrastructure** — Built on battle-tested Aave V3 smart contracts, inheriting robust risk management, liquidation mechanisms, and auditability.
- **Non-Custodial** — All positions are managed through transparent, permissionless smart contracts with no central custodian holding user funds.
- **Institutional Focus** — Tailored risk parameters and supported asset types are oriented toward institutional-grade participants and compliance requirements.
- **Ethereum Native** — Deployed on Ethereum mainnet, benefiting from network security, composability, and the broader DeFi ecosystem.
