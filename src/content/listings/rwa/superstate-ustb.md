## Getting Started

1. **Verify eligibility** — USTB is restricted to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; confirm your status before applying.
2. **Contact Superstate** — Reach out through the official Superstate website to begin the onboarding and KYC/AML process.
3. **Complete subscription documents** — As a private fund, investors must complete the required legal and compliance documentation.
4. **Receive on-chain shares** — Once approved, USTB tokens (ERC-20) are issued to your Ethereum-compatible wallet, representing your fund shares.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized on Ethereum** — Fund shares exist as ERC-20 tokens, enabling on-chain transfer and composability within DeFi infrastructure.
- **Short-duration Treasury exposure** — Invests in U.S. Treasury Bills and Government Agency securities, targeting capital preservation with yield.
- **Regulated structure** — Operates as a private fund under U.S. securities regulations, offering a compliant path to tokenized yield.
- **Institutional focus** — Designed exclusively for Qualified Purchasers, maintaining the regulatory standards expected by institutional investors.
- **On-chain settlement** — Blockchain-based share issuance enables faster, more transparent settlement compared to traditional fund structures.
