## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — USTB is available only to qualified purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; complete Superstate's onboarding and KYC/AML process.
2. **Connect and subscribe** — Once approved, connect an Ethereum wallet and subscribe to the fund through the Superstate platform using USDC or approved stablecoins.
3. **Receive USTB tokens** — ERC-20 USTB tokens are issued to your wallet, representing your pro-rata share of the underlying government securities portfolio.
4. **Monitor and redeem** — Track yield accrual and initiate redemptions back to USDC through the Superstate interface subject to fund terms.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized T-Bills** — Exposure to short-duration U.S. Treasury bills and agency securities represented as an ERC-20 token on Ethereum.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — The fund is designed to track yield close to the prevailing federal funds rate, offering competitive returns on-chain.
- **Institutional-Grade Structure** — Built for qualified purchasers with regulatory compliance, KYC/AML, and proper fund accounting baked in.
- **On-Chain Composability** — USTB tokens are standard ERC-20 assets, enabling potential integration with DeFi protocols and smart contract workflows.
- **USDC Settlement** — Subscriptions and redemptions are handled in USDC, reducing friction between traditional finance and on-chain operations.
