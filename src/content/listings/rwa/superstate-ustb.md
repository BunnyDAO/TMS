## Getting Started

1. **Verify eligibility** — Confirm you meet the Qualified Purchaser requirements under U.S. securities law before applying.
2. **Apply on Superstate** — Visit superstate.com and complete the onboarding and KYC/AML process through their platform.
3. **Fund your account** — Once approved, transfer funds to subscribe to USTB shares, which are represented as tokens on Ethereum.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your tokenized Treasury holdings and accrued yield directly on-chain or through the Superstate dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized T-Bills** — Holds short-duration U.S. Treasury bills and government agency securities represented as ERC-20 tokens on Ethereum.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — Fund strategy targets yields near the prevailing federal funds rate, offering a competitive risk-adjusted return.
- **Qualified Purchaser Access** — Designed specifically for institutional and high-net-worth investors meeting Qualified Purchaser standards.
- **On-Chain Settlement** — Ownership and transfers are recorded on Ethereum, enabling composability with DeFi protocols where permitted.
- **Regulated Structure** — Operates as a registered investment fund, combining traditional regulatory compliance with blockchain-native infrastructure.
