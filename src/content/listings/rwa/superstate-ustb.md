## Getting Started

1. **Verify eligibility** — Confirm you meet the Qualified Purchaser requirements under U.S. securities law before applying.
2. **Apply on Superstate** — Visit superstate.com and complete the onboarding and KYC/AML process for institutional access.
3. **Connect your wallet** — Link an Ethereum-compatible wallet to receive and hold USTB ERC-20 tokens.
4. **Subscribe to the fund** — Deposit approved assets to receive USTB tokens representing your pro-rata share of the underlying U.S. government securities.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized T-Bills** — Provides on-chain exposure to short-duration U.S. Treasury bills and agency securities via an ERC-20 token.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — The fund targets returns tied to the federal funds rate, offering competitive yield relative to money market alternatives.
- **Ethereum Native** — USTB tokens are standard ERC-20 assets, enabling composability with DeFi protocols and on-chain settlement.
- **Institutional Grade** — Restricted to Qualified Purchasers, ensuring regulatory compliance and institutional-level custody and operations.
- **Significant TVL** — Over $732M in total value locked, reflecting strong institutional adoption of the product.
- **Modernized Fund Access** — Superstate's infrastructure removes traditional friction from fund subscriptions and redemptions through blockchain-based settlement.
