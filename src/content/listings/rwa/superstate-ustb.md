## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — USTB is restricted to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; verify your status before applying.
2. **Create an account** — Visit superstate.com and complete the onboarding and KYC/AML process required for institutional access.
3. **Connect a wallet** — Link an Ethereum-compatible wallet to receive the ERC-20 USTB token upon subscription.
4. **Subscribe and hold** — Deposit funds through the platform and receive USTB tokens representing your share of the underlying Treasury portfolio.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized T-Bills** — Holds short-duration U.S. Treasury Bills and Government Agency securities, providing low-risk yield on-chain.
- **ERC-20 on Ethereum** — Fund shares are represented as standard ERC-20 tokens, enabling composability with Ethereum-based protocols.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — Yield is designed to track the federal funds rate, offering predictable returns aligned with monetary policy.
- **Qualified Purchaser Access** — Designed for institutional and high-net-worth investors meeting the Qualified Purchaser threshold.
- **Regulated Structure** — Operates within a regulated fund structure, combining traditional finance compliance with blockchain-native settlement.
