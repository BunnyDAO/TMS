## Getting Started

1. **Verify eligibility** — USTB is restricted to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; confirm your status before applying.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Apply through Superstate's platform, completing KYC/AML and investor accreditation verification.
3. **Subscribe to the fund** — Once approved, subscribe and receive USTB ERC-20 tokens representing your fund shares on Ethereum.
4. **Manage on-chain** — Hold or transfer your USTB tokens via a compatible Ethereum wallet, with yield accruing based on the fund's T-Bill holdings.

## Key Features

- **ERC-20 Token Representation** — Fund shares are issued as Ethereum ERC-20 tokens, enabling on-chain transferability and composability.
- **U.S. Treasury Exposure** — Invests in short-duration Treasury Bills and Government Agency securities for capital preservation and yield.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — The fund is structured to deliver returns tracking near the prevailing federal funds rate.
- **Qualified Purchaser Access** — Designed for institutional and high-net-worth investors meeting the Qualified Purchaser threshold under the Investment Company Act.
- **Blockchain-Native Infrastructure** — Built by Superstate to modernize fund administration and settlement using tokenized financial products on Ethereum.
