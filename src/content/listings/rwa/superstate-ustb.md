## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — USTB is restricted to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; confirm your status before applying.
2. **Contact Superstate** — Reach out via the Superstate website to begin the onboarding and KYC/AML process.
3. **Complete onboarding** — Submit required documentation and complete the institutional account setup with Superstate's team.
4. **Receive USTB tokens** — Once approved, receive ERC-20 USTB tokens on Ethereum representing your fund shares and begin accruing yield.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasury Exposure** — Provides on-chain access to short-duration U.S. Treasury Bills and Government Agency securities via an ERC-20 token.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — The fund targets returns in line with the federal funds rate, offering competitive yield for institutional capital.
- **Ethereum Native** — Issued as an ERC-20 token, enabling composability with Ethereum-based infrastructure and on-chain settlement.
- **Qualified Purchaser Access** — Structured as a regulated fund product available only to institutional-grade Qualified Purchasers, maintaining compliance with U.S. securities law.
- **Regulated Fund Structure** — Built by Superstate, a firm focused on bringing traditional financial products into blockchain rails with proper regulatory framing.
