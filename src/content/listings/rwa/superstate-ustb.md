## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — Confirm you meet the Qualified Purchaser requirements under U.S. securities law before applying.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Visit superstate.com and go through the KYC/AML verification and account setup process.
3. **Subscribe to the fund** — Once approved, subscribe to the USTB fund and receive ERC-20 tokens representing your shares.
4. **Hold or transfer on-chain** — Use your USTB tokens within compatible Ethereum wallets or protocols while accruing T-bill yield.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized T-Bills** — Holds short-duration U.S. Treasury Bills, providing exposure to one of the safest fixed-income instruments available.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — Fund returns are designed to track closely with the federal funds rate, offering competitive short-term yield.
- **ERC-20 Standard** — USTB tokens conform to the ERC-20 standard, enabling compatibility with Ethereum wallets and DeFi infrastructure.
- **Institutional Grade** — Access is restricted to Qualified Purchasers, ensuring regulatory compliance for institutional and high-net-worth participants.
- **On-Chain Transferability** — Token holders can transfer shares on-chain, unlocking liquidity and composability not available with traditional fund structures.
