## Getting Started

1. **Verify eligibility** — USTB is restricted to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; confirm your status before applying.
2. **Contact Superstate** — Reach out via the Superstate website to begin the onboarding and KYC/AML process.
3. **Receive USTB tokens** — Once approved and funded, USTB ERC-20 tokens representing your fund shares are issued to your Ethereum wallet.
4. **Monitor yield** — Track your on-chain position and accruing yield targeting the federal funds rate through your wallet or Superstate's platform.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized T-Bills** — Invests in short-duration U.S. Treasury Bills and Government Agency securities, providing low-risk yield on-chain.
- **ERC-20 on Ethereum** — Fund shares are represented as standard ERC-20 tokens, enabling on-chain transferability and composability.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — Yield is designed to track the federal funds rate, offering a competitive return for institutional cash management.
- **Qualified Purchaser Access** — Structured as a private fund for institutional and high-net-worth investors meeting Qualified Purchaser thresholds.
- **Regulated Structure** — Operates within established U.S. securities regulations, providing a compliant path to on-chain government securities exposure.
- **Institutional-Grade Custody** — Underlying assets are held with regulated custodians, maintaining the security standards expected by institutional investors.
