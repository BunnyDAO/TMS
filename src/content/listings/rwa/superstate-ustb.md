## Getting Started

1. **Verify eligibility** — USTB is restricted to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; confirm your status before proceeding.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Visit superstate.com and go through the required KYC/AML verification process to gain access to the fund.
3. **Fund your account** — Once approved, follow Superstate's instructions to deposit capital and receive USTB tokens representing your fund shares.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your on-chain USTB balance and accruing yield directly via your Ethereum wallet or supported DeFi dashboards.

## Key Features

- **ERC-20 Token** — USTB shares are represented as standard Ethereum ERC-20 tokens, enabling on-chain transferability and composability.
- **T-Bill Exposure** — The fund invests in short-duration U.S. Treasury Bills, offering a low-risk, government-backed yield source.
- **Federal Funds Rate Target** — Returns are designed to track in line with the prevailing federal funds rate, offering predictable yield.
- **Qualified Purchaser Access** — Designed specifically for institutional and high-net-worth investors meeting Qualified Purchaser thresholds.
- **On-Chain Settlement** — Leverages Ethereum's blockchain infrastructure for transparent, auditable fund operations and token management.
- **Institutional Grade** — Built by Superstate, a firm focused on bridging traditional finance with blockchain-based investment products.
