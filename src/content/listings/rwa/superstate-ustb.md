## Getting Started

1. **Verify eligibility** — USTB is restricted to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; confirm your status before applying.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Contact Superstate directly via their website to begin KYC/AML verification and account setup.
3. **Subscribe to the fund** — Once approved, subscribe and receive USTB ERC-20 tokens representing your share of the underlying government securities.
4. **Manage on-chain** — Hold, transfer, or integrate USTB tokens within eligible DeFi protocols or custody solutions that support whitelisted RWA tokens.

## Key Features

- **T-Bill Exposure** — Invests in short-duration U.S. Treasury bills and agency securities, providing low-risk government-backed yield.
- **ERC-20 Token** — Fund shares are represented as an ERC-20 token on Ethereum, enabling on-chain settlement and programmability.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — The fund targets yield aligned with the federal funds rate, offering competitive returns relative to cash equivalents.
- **Institutional Grade** — Designed for Qualified Purchasers, with regulatory compliance and institutional-level custody standards.
- **On-Chain Settlement** — Token-based structure enables faster, more transparent settlement compared to traditional fund infrastructure.
- **Modernized Investing** — Part of Superstate's broader mission to bring regulated financial products onto public blockchain rails.
