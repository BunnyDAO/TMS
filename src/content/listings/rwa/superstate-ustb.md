## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — USTB is restricted to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; verify your status before applying.
2. **Apply via Superstate** — Visit superstate.com and complete the onboarding and KYC/AML process required for fund participation.
3. **Fund your account** — Once approved, deposit funds through the supported channels to subscribe to the USTB fund.
4. **Receive ERC-20 tokens** — Upon subscription, USTB tokens representing your fund shares are issued to your Ethereum wallet on-chain.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized T-Bills** — Invests in short-duration U.S. government securities, providing yield-bearing exposure with on-chain representation.
- **ERC-20 Standard** — Fund shares exist as standard Ethereum tokens, enabling composability with DeFi protocols and on-chain transfers.
- **Regulatory Compliance** — Structured as a private fund under U.S. securities regulations, with access limited to Qualified Purchasers.
- **Institutional Grade** — Designed for institutional and sophisticated investors seeking compliant, blockchain-native fixed-income exposure.
- **On-Chain Settlement** — Leverages Ethereum's infrastructure for transparent, programmable asset management and transfer.
