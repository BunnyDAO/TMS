## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — USTB is restricted to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; verify your eligibility before applying.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Visit superstate.com and go through the KYC/AML onboarding process required for institutional investors.
3. **Receive USTB tokens** — Once approved, receive ERC-20 USTB tokens in your whitelisted Ethereum wallet representing your fund shares.
4. **Monitor yield** — Track your position on-chain or via the Superstate dashboard as yield accrues targeting the federal funds rate.

## Key Features

- **ERC-20 Token** — Fund shares are represented as a standard Ethereum ERC-20 token, enabling on-chain composability and transparent ownership.
- **T-Bill Backing** — The fund holds short-duration U.S. Treasury bills and agency securities, providing direct exposure to government debt.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — Yield is structured to track closely with the federal funds rate, offering competitive returns relative to cash equivalents.
- **Institutional Access** — Designed specifically for Qualified Purchasers, ensuring regulatory compliance for institutional and high-net-worth participants.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Holdings and token supply are verifiable on the Ethereum blockchain, offering a level of transparency not found in traditional funds.
- **Regulated Structure** — Superstate operates within existing U.S. securities regulations, bridging traditional finance compliance with blockchain-native infrastructure.
