## Getting Started

1. **Verify eligibility** — USTB is restricted to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; confirm you meet the requirements before applying.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Submit KYC/AML documentation through Superstate's platform to gain access to the fund.
3. **Connect your wallet** — Link a compatible Ethereum wallet to receive and manage your USTB ERC-20 tokens.
4. **Subscribe to the fund** — Deposit eligible assets to receive USTB tokens representing your proportional share of the underlying Treasury portfolio.

## Key Features

- **ERC-20 Token Wrapper** — Fund shares are represented as an ERC-20 token on Ethereum, enabling on-chain transferability and composability.
- **Short-Duration T-Bill Exposure** — The fund invests in short-duration U.S. Treasury Bills and government agency securities, prioritizing capital preservation and yield.
- **Qualified Purchaser Access** — Structured as a private fund for institutional and high-net-worth investors meeting Qualified Purchaser thresholds.
- **Regulated Structure** — Operates within established U.S. securities regulations, offering a compliant path to tokenized government securities.
- **On-Chain Settlement** — Leverages Ethereum's infrastructure for transparent, programmable ownership and potential DeFi integrations.
