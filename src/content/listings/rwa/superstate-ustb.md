## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — USTB is restricted to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; confirm your status before applying.
2. **Apply on Superstate** — Visit superstate.com and complete the onboarding process, including KYC/AML verification.
3. **Connect your wallet** — Once approved, link an Ethereum-compatible wallet to receive and manage your USTB tokens.
4. **Subscribe to the fund** — Deposit eligible assets to receive ERC-20 USTB tokens representing your share of the underlying Treasury Bill portfolio.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized T-Bills** — Invests in short-duration U.S. Government Securities, providing on-chain exposure to one of the world's safest asset classes.
- **ERC-20 Standard** — USTB tokens conform to the ERC-20 standard, enabling composability with Ethereum-based protocols and wallets.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — The fund targets returns in line with the federal funds rate, offering competitive short-duration yield.
- **Institutional Grade** — Designed exclusively for Qualified Purchasers, meeting strict regulatory and compliance standards.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Token holdings and transfers are recorded on Ethereum, providing verifiable and auditable ownership records.
- **Modernized Investing** — Superstate bridges traditional finance and DeFi infrastructure, reducing friction for institutional participants.
