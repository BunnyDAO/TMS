## Getting Started

1. **Verify eligibility** — Confirm you meet the Qualified Purchaser requirements under U.S. securities law (generally $5M+ in investments).
2. **Apply on Superstate** — Visit superstate.com and complete the institutional onboarding and KYC/AML process.
3. **Connect your wallet** — Link a compatible Ethereum wallet to receive and hold USTB tokens once approved.
4. **Subscribe to the fund** — Deposit approved assets to receive USTB tokens representing your pro-rata share of the underlying government securities.

## Key Features

- **ERC-20 Token** — USTB is a standard Ethereum token, enabling composability with on-chain protocols and custodians.
- **T-Bill Exposure** — The fund invests in short-duration U.S. Treasury Bills and Government Agency securities.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — Designed to track returns in line with the prevailing federal funds rate.
- **Qualified Purchaser Access** — Structured under U.S. securities regulations for institutional and high-net-worth investors.
- **On-Chain Settlement** — Token transfers and redemptions occur on Ethereum, reducing settlement friction versus traditional fund structures.
