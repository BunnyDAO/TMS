## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — Confirm you qualify as a Qualified Purchaser under U.S. securities law, as USTB is restricted to eligible investors.
2. **Apply on Superstate** — Visit superstate.com and complete the onboarding and KYC/AML process to gain access to the fund.
3. **Connect your wallet** — Link an Ethereum-compatible wallet to receive and hold USTB ERC-20 tokens.
4. **Subscribe to the fund** — Deposit approved funds to receive USTB tokens representing your share of the underlying U.S. Government Securities.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasuries** — USTB represents ownership in a short-duration U.S. Government Securities Fund, bringing Treasury exposure directly on-chain as an ERC-20 token.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — The fund targets returns in line with the federal funds rate, providing a yield benchmark familiar to traditional finance investors.
- **Ethereum-Native** — Built on Ethereum, USTB can interact with DeFi protocols and be held in self-custodied wallets.
- **Regulated Structure** — Superstate operates within a regulated framework, offering institutional-grade compliance around a blockchain-based product.
- **Qualified Purchaser Access** — Restricted to Qualified Purchasers, ensuring regulatory compliance while serving sophisticated investors seeking on-chain yield.
