## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — USTB is available exclusively to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; verify your status before applying.
2. **Contact Superstate** — Reach out through the official Superstate website to begin the onboarding and KYC/AML process.
3. **Complete fund subscription** — Submit the required documentation to subscribe to the USTB private fund.
4. **Receive tokenized shares** — Once onboarded, holdings are represented as on-chain tokens on Ethereum, enabling transparent and programmable ownership.

## Key Features

- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — USTB seeks to generate returns in line with the prevailing federal funds rate through exposure to U.S. Treasury securities.
- **Ethereum-Native** — Fund shares are tokenized on Ethereum, allowing on-chain settlement and composability with the broader DeFi ecosystem.
- **Institutional Access** — Designed for Qualified Purchasers, providing an institutional-grade product with regulatory compliance built in.
- **Tokenized Ownership** — Investors hold on-chain representations of their fund shares, enabling transparent record-keeping and potential future integrations.
- **Private Fund Structure** — Operates as a private fund, maintaining familiar legal structures while leveraging blockchain for modernized fund administration.
