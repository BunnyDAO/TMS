## Getting Started

1. **Verify eligibility** — USTB is restricted to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; confirm your status before applying.
2. **Contact Superstate** — Reach out via the Superstate website to begin the onboarding and KYC/AML process.
3. **Complete subscription documents** — As a private fund, investors must complete standard fund subscription agreements.
4. **Receive USTB tokens** — Upon acceptance, fund shares are issued as ERC-20 tokens to your whitelisted Ethereum wallet.

## Key Features

- **ERC-20 Token Representation** — Fund shares exist as standard Ethereum tokens, enabling on-chain transfers and portfolio accounting.
- **Short-Duration Treasury Exposure** — Invests in U.S. Treasury Bills and Government Agency securities, targeting capital preservation and liquidity.
- **Institutional-Grade Structure** — Operates as a regulated private fund with proper legal and compliance infrastructure for institutional investors.
- **Qualified Purchaser Access** — Designed specifically for institutional and high-net-worth investors meeting the Qualified Purchaser threshold.
- **On-Chain Settlement** — Leverages Ethereum's programmable infrastructure to streamline transfers and reduce settlement friction.
- **Modernized Fund Infrastructure** — Part of Superstate's broader mission to bring traditional financial products onto blockchain rails.
