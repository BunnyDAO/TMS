## Getting Started

1. **Verify eligibility** — Confirm you meet the Qualified Purchaser requirements under U.S. securities law before applying.
2. **Contact Superstate** — Reach out via the Superstate website to begin the onboarding and KYC/AML process.
3. **Fund your account** — Once approved, transfer assets to subscribe to USTB shares represented as ERC-20 tokens on Ethereum.
4. **Manage on-chain** — Hold or transfer your USTB tokens through any compatible Ethereum wallet, subject to transfer restrictions.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Government Securities** — Provides on-chain exposure to a portfolio of short-duration U.S. government securities via an ERC-20 token.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — The fund is managed to target yields competitive with the prevailing federal funds rate.
- **Ethereum-Native** — USTB tokens exist natively on Ethereum, enabling composability with DeFi protocols where permitted.
- **Qualified Purchaser Access** — Restricted to Qualified Purchasers, ensuring regulatory compliance for institutional and high-net-worth participants.
- **Modernized Settlement** — Leverages blockchain infrastructure to streamline fund administration and ownership transfer compared to traditional funds.
