## Getting Started

1. **Verify eligibility** — USTB is restricted to Qualified Purchasers as defined under U.S. securities law; confirm your status before proceeding.
2. **Contact Superstate** — Reach out via the Superstate website to begin the onboarding and KYC/AML process required for institutional participation.
3. **Complete compliance requirements** — Submit the necessary documentation to satisfy regulatory requirements for access to the fund.
4. **Receive USTB tokens** — Once approved, receive ERC-20 USTB tokens in your whitelisted Ethereum wallet representing your fund shares.

## Key Features

- **ERC-20 Tokenization** — Fund shares are represented as ERC-20 tokens on Ethereum, enabling on-chain transferability and composability.
- **Short-Duration Focus** — Invests in short-duration U.S. T-Bills and government agency securities, minimizing interest rate risk.
- **Federal Funds Rate Targeting** — Yield is designed to track the federal funds rate, offering a competitive return relative to traditional cash management.
- **Institutional-Grade Compliance** — Restricted to Qualified Purchasers with full KYC/AML onboarding, meeting regulatory standards.
- **On-Chain Settlement** — Leverages Ethereum's infrastructure for transparent, programmable settlement of fund activity.
- **Stablecoin Alternative** — Provides a yield-bearing on-chain asset that can serve as a productive alternative to holding stablecoins.
