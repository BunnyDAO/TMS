## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit the OpenEden portal and connect a compatible Web3 wallet to access the TBILL vault interface.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Go through the required KYC/AML verification process to meet institutional compliance requirements.
3. **Deposit USDC** — Fund your position by depositing USDC into the TBILL vault to receive tokenized T-bill exposure.
4. **Track yield** — Monitor your accruing yield and manage your position directly through the DeFi portal dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Moody's A-Rated Vault** — The smart contract vault carries a Moody's A rating, providing institutional-grade credit assurance uncommon in DeFi.
- **BNY Mellon Custody** — BNY Mellon acts as both Manager and Custodian, ensuring the underlying T-bills are held by a globally trusted financial institution.
- **24/7 On-Chain Access** — Unlike traditional T-bill investments, TBILL allows minting and redemption at any time without being bound by market hours.
- **U.S. Treasury Bill Backing** — Yield is derived directly from short-duration U.S. Treasury Bills, one of the lowest-risk fixed income instruments available.
- **Tokenized Yield** — TBILL tokens represent a claim on the underlying T-bill portfolio, making the yield composable and transferable within DeFi ecosystems.
