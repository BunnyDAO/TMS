## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit the OpenEden portal and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet to begin onboarding.
2. **Complete KYC/AML verification** — As an institutional product, users must complete identity verification before accessing the vault.
3. **Deposit USDC** — Fund your position by depositing USDC into the TBILL vault smart contract.
4. **Receive TBILL tokens** — You will receive TBILL tokens representing your proportional share of the underlying U.S. Treasury Bills and begin accruing yield.

## Key Features

- **Moody's A-Rated Vault** — The smart contract has received an A rating from Moody's, providing a measurable credit quality benchmark for institutional investors.
- **BNY Mellon as Custodian** — BNY Mellon serves as both Manager and Custodian of the underlying assets, ensuring institutional-grade asset safekeeping.
- **24/7 On-Chain Access** — Unlike traditional T-bill products, TBILL allows deposits and redemptions at any time through the Ethereum blockchain.
- **Tokenized T-Bill Exposure** — Investors gain yield-bearing exposure to U.S. Treasury Bills in a fully tokenized, transferable ERC-20 format.
- **Ethereum-Native** — Built on Ethereum, enabling composability with the broader DeFi ecosystem and transparent on-chain auditability.
