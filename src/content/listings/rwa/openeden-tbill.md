## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit the OpenEden portal and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Complete KYC/onboarding** — As an institutional-grade product, users must complete identity verification before depositing.
3. **Deposit stablecoins** — Deposit USDC into the TBILL vault to receive TBILL tokens representing your share of the underlying T-bill exposure.
4. **Earn yield** — Hold TBILL tokens to accrue U.S. Treasury Bill yields, redeemable on-chain at any time.

## Key Features

- **Moody's A-Rated Vault** — The smart contract vault carries a Moody's A rating, providing institutional-grade credit assurance rarely seen in DeFi.
- **BNY Mellon Custody** — BNY Mellon acts as both Manager and Custodian, ensuring the underlying T-bills are held with a globally recognized financial institution.
- **24/7 Accessibility** — Unlike traditional T-bill markets, the vault allows deposits and redemptions around the clock via blockchain infrastructure.
- **ERC-20 Token** — TBILL is a standard ERC-20 token, making it composable with other DeFi protocols and transferable between wallets.
- **Direct T-Bill Exposure** — Investors gain real yield from U.S. Treasury Bills rather than synthetic or derivative instruments.
- **Stablecoin Entry Point** — USDC is used as the deposit currency, lowering friction for existing stablecoin holders seeking yield.
