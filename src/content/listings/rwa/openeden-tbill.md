## Getting Started

1. **Connect a wallet** — Visit openeden.com and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet to begin the onboarding process.
2. **Complete KYC/KYB** — As an institutional or qualified investor product, you will need to complete identity verification and eligibility checks.
3. **Deposit funds** — Once approved, deposit USDC or other accepted stablecoins into the TBILL vault smart contract.
4. **Receive TBILL tokens** — Your deposit is deployed into U.S. Treasury Bills and you receive TBILL tokens representing your yield-bearing position.

## Key Features

- **Moody's A-rated Vault** — The smart contract vault carries a Moody's A rating, providing institutional-grade credit assurance.
- **BNY as Manager & Custodian** — Bank of New York Mellon oversees asset management and custody, ensuring regulatory-grade safekeeping.
- **24/7 On-Chain Access** — Unlike traditional T-bill investments, the vault operates continuously on Ethereum, enabling near-instant subscriptions and redemptions.
- **Direct T-Bill Exposure** — Funds are invested directly into U.S. Treasury Bills, not money market funds or synthetic instruments.
- **Ethereum-Native** — TBILL tokens are ERC-20 compatible, enabling composability with DeFi protocols and on-chain portfolio management.
- **Yield Accrual** — Investors earn T-bill yields reflected in the value of their TBILL token holdings over time.
