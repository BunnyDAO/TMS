## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit the OpenEden portal and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet to begin the onboarding process.
2. **Complete KYC/KYB** — As an institutional-grade product, you will need to complete identity and eligibility verification before accessing the vault.
3. **Deposit USDC** — Once approved, deposit USDC into the TBILL vault to receive tokenized T-bill exposure.
4. **Monitor and redeem** — Track your yield accrual on-chain and redeem at any time, 24/7, without waiting for traditional market hours.
