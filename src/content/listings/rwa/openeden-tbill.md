## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit the OpenEden portal and connect a compatible Web3 wallet to access the TBILL vault.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Complete any required KYC/AML verification steps to qualify for access as an eligible investor.
3. **Deposit USDC** — Deposit USDC into the TBILL vault to receive tokenized exposure to U.S. Treasury Bills.
4. **Earn yield** — Your position accrues T-bill yield on-chain, redeemable 24/7 directly through the smart contract.

## Key Features

- **Moody's A-Rated Vault** — The smart contract vault carries a Moody's A rating, providing institutional-grade credit assurance.
- **BNY as Manager & Custodian** — Bank of New York Mellon oversees management and custody of the underlying T-bill assets.
- **24/7 Access** — Unlike traditional treasury products, deposits and redemptions are available around the clock via on-chain transactions.
- **Real T-Bill Backing** — Each token is backed 1:1 by actual U.S. Treasury Bills held in custody, not synthetic exposure.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Holdings and vault activity are verifiable on-chain at any time, offering real-time auditability.
- **Stablecoin Alternative** — TBILL tokens offer a yield-bearing alternative to holding stablecoins idle in DeFi portfolios.
