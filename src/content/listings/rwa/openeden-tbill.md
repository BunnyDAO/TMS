## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit the OpenEden portal and connect a compatible Web3 wallet to access the TBILL vault interface.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Go through the KYC/AML verification process required for institutional and accredited investor access.
3. **Deposit USDC** — Deposit USDC into the TBILL vault to mint TBILL tokens representing your proportional share of the underlying Treasury Bills.
4. **Earn yield** — Hold TBILL tokens to accrue yield from the underlying U.S. Treasury Bills, redeemable 24/7 on-chain.

## Key Features

- **Moody's A-Rated Vault** — The smart contract vault carries a Moody's A rating, providing institutional-grade credit assurance for on-chain T-bill exposure.
- **BNY Mellon Custody** — BNY Mellon acts as both manager and custodian of the underlying assets, ensuring regulated and secure asset management.
- **24/7 Accessibility** — Unlike traditional Treasury Bill markets, TBILL offers around-the-clock minting and redemption via smart contracts.
- **$168M TVL** — A substantial total value locked reflects significant institutional and DeFi community adoption of the product.
- **USDC Integration** — Seamless entry and exit using USDC keeps the experience familiar for stablecoin-native DeFi users.
- **Tokenized Ownership** — TBILL tokens represent direct, verifiable on-chain claims to U.S. government debt instruments held in custody.
