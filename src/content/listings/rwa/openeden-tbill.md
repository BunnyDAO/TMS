## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit the OpenEden portal and connect a compatible Web3 wallet to access the TBILL vault.
2. **Complete KYC/onboarding** — As an institutional-grade product, users must complete identity verification and eligibility checks before depositing.
3. **Deposit stablecoins** — Fund your position by depositing supported stablecoins to mint TBILL tokens representing your share of the underlying T-Bill holdings.
4. **Earn yield** — Your TBILL tokens accrue yield reflecting current U.S. Treasury Bill rates, redeemable 24/7 on-chain.

## Key Features

- **Moody's A-Rated Vault** — One of the few tokenized treasury products with a formal credit rating, providing institutional-grade risk assurance.
- **BNY Mellon as Manager & Custodian** — Backed by one of the world's largest custodian banks, ensuring secure and compliant asset management.
- **24/7 On-Chain Access** — Unlike traditional T-Bill markets, TBILL allows continuous minting and redemption without waiting for market hours.
- **Transparent TVL** — Over $168M in total value locked, verifiable on-chain at any time.
- **DeFi Composability** — TBILL tokens can potentially be integrated into broader DeFi protocols as yield-bearing collateral or liquidity.
- **Regulated Structure** — Designed to meet institutional compliance requirements, bridging traditional finance regulations with blockchain infrastructure.
