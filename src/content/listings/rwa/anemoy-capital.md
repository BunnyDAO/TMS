## Getting Started

1. **Visit the platform** — Go to [anemoy.io](https://www.anemoy.io) to explore available tokenized asset funds.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Institutional investors go through KYC/AML verification before accessing funds.
3. **Select a product** — Choose from offerings like the Liquid Treasury Fund or other RWA credit products.
4. **Invest on-chain** — Allocate capital and receive tokenized fund shares representing your position directly in your Web3 wallet.

## Key Features

- **Liquid Treasury Fund** — A tokenized fund with $1.2B+ TVL providing on-chain exposure to U.S. Treasury bills and short-duration instruments.
- **Centrifuge Integration** — Built on Centrifuge's infrastructure, enabling transparent, blockchain-native asset origination and management.
- **Institutional-Grade Credit** — Offers structured credit products meeting institutional risk and compliance standards.
- **Full Lifecycle Management** — Handles everything from asset origination to on-chain distribution within a single platform.
- **DeFi Composability** — Tokenized positions can interact with broader DeFi protocols, enabling use as collateral or in liquidity strategies.
- **Real-World Asset Diversity** — Covers multiple RWA categories including Treasuries, private credit, and other yield-bearing instruments.
