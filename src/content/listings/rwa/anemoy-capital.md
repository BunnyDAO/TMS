## Getting Started

1. **Visit the platform** — Go to [anemoy.io](https://www.anemoy.io) to explore available tokenized asset products.
2. **Check eligibility** — Anemoy products are institutional-grade; review any KYC/AML or accreditation requirements for the specific fund you're interested in.
3. **Connect via Centrifuge** — Access Anemoy's tokenized pools through the Centrifuge protocol using a compatible Web3 wallet.
4. **Invest on-chain** — Allocate capital to tokenized Treasuries, credit, or other real-world asset products and receive on-chain yield.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasuries** — On-chain access to U.S. Treasury exposure structured for DeFi-native investors and protocols.
- **Centrifuge-Powered** — Built on the Centrifuge protocol, providing a proven infrastructure for real-world asset tokenization and liquidity.
- **Full Lifecycle Management** — Covers the entire process from asset origination through to on-chain distribution, reducing counterparty complexity.
- **Institutional-Grade Credit** — Offers structured credit products meeting institutional standards while remaining accessible in DeFi.
- **Broad Asset Class Coverage** — Spans multiple real-world asset categories beyond Treasuries, including private credit and other yield-bearing instruments.
- **Transparent On-Chain Accounting** — All positions and cash flows are recorded on-chain, enabling real-time auditability and composability.
