## Getting Started

1. **Visit Anemoy** — Go to [anemoy.io](https://www.anemoy.io) and explore the available tokenized asset products.
2. **Connect your wallet** — Use a compatible Web3 wallet to access on-chain fund offerings through the Centrifuge protocol.
3. **Complete onboarding** — As an institutional or qualified investor, complete any required KYC/AML verification to access funds.
4. **Invest on-chain** — Allocate capital into tokenized credit, Treasury, or yield products and receive on-chain representations of your position.

## Key Features

- **Centrifuge-powered infrastructure** — Built on Centrifuge, a leading RWA protocol, ensuring transparent and auditable on-chain asset management.
- **Institutional-grade products** — Offers credit, Treasuries, and yield instruments structured to meet institutional standards within a DeFi environment.
- **Full lifecycle management** — Covers asset origination, structuring, and distribution entirely on-chain, reducing reliance on traditional intermediaries.
- **Broad RWA exposure** — Provides access to a diverse spectrum of real-world assets, not limited to a single asset class or geography.
- **DeFi composability** — Tokenized fund positions can potentially integrate with broader DeFi protocols, enabling use as collateral or in yield strategies.
