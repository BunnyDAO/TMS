## Getting Started

1. **Visit the platform** — Go to [anemoy.io](https://www.anemoy.io) to explore available tokenized asset pools and funds.
2. **Connect your wallet** — Use a compatible Web3 wallet to access the on-chain investment interface powered by Centrifuge.
3. **Complete onboarding** — Institutional investors may need to complete KYC/AML verification before accessing certain funds.
4. **Invest in a pool** — Select a tokenized asset pool (e.g., Liquid Treasury Fund or credit pool) and deposit supported stablecoins to receive yield-bearing tokens.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasuries** — On-chain exposure to U.S. Treasury bills and other government securities for DeFi-native yield.
- **Institutional-Grade Credit** — Access to tokenized private credit and structured finance products typically reserved for institutional investors.
- **Centrifuge Infrastructure** — Built on Centrifuge's battle-tested RWA protocol, enabling transparent, on-chain asset management from origination to distribution.
- **Full On-Chain Lifecycle** — Asset origination, structuring, and distribution are handled entirely on-chain, reducing counterparty and settlement risk.
- **DeFi Composability** — Tokenized fund shares can integrate with broader DeFi protocols for lending, collateral, and liquidity use cases.
- **Broad Asset Spectrum** — Covers a range of real-world asset classes beyond just Treasuries, including diversified credit strategies.
