## Getting Started

1. **Visit Anemoy** — Go to [anemoy.io](https://www.anemoy.io) and review available tokenized asset pools and fund offerings.
2. **Check eligibility** — Anemoy products are institutional-grade; review KYC/AML and investor qualification requirements before proceeding.
3. **Connect via Centrifuge** — Anemoy operates on the Centrifuge protocol, so you will interact through a compatible Web3 wallet to access on-chain pools.
4. **Invest in a pool** — Select a fund or asset pool (e.g., tokenized Treasuries or credit) and follow the on-chain subscription process.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasuries** — Provides on-chain access to U.S. Treasury bill exposure through tokenized fund structures.
- **Centrifuge-Powered** — Built on the Centrifuge protocol, leveraging its infrastructure for RWA origination, structuring, and on-chain distribution.
- **Institutional-Grade Credit** — Offers exposure to private credit and other structured real-world asset classes beyond just government securities.
- **Full Lifecycle Management** — Covers origination through distribution on-chain, reducing reliance on traditional off-chain intermediaries.
- **DeFi Composability** — Tokenized positions can potentially integrate with broader DeFi protocols, enabling yield-bearing assets to be used across decentralized finance.
- **Compliant Structure** — Designed with regulatory compliance in mind, including investor verification processes suited to institutional participants.
