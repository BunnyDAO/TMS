## Getting Started

1. **Visit the platform** — Go to [anemoy.io](https://www.anemoy.io) to explore available tokenized asset products.
2. **Review available funds** — Browse offerings such as the Liquid Treasury Fund (LTF) and other RWA pools built on Centrifuge.
3. **Complete onboarding** — Institutional investors can connect a compatible wallet and complete KYC/AML verification as required.
4. **Invest on-chain** — Allocate capital to your chosen fund and receive tokenized fund shares representing your on-chain position.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasuries** — On-chain access to U.S. Treasury bill exposure through institutional-grade fund structures.
- **Centrifuge-Powered** — Built on Centrifuge's RWA infrastructure, enabling transparent, auditable on-chain asset management.
- **Full Lifecycle Management** — Covers origination, structuring, and distribution of real-world assets within a single platform.
- **DeFi Composability** — Tokenized fund shares can interact with DeFi protocols, enabling broader capital efficiency.
- **Institutional-Grade Credit** — Access to structured credit products alongside Treasuries for diversified RWA exposure.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — All fund activity is recorded on-chain, providing verifiable and auditable investment records.
