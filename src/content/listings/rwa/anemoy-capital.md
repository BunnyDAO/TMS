## Getting Started

1. **Visit the platform** — Go to [anemoy.io](https://www.anemoy.io) and review the available tokenized asset products, including their Liquid Treasury Fund.
2. **Complete onboarding** — As an institutional or accredited investor, complete the required KYC/AML verification process to gain access.
3. **Connect your wallet** — Link a compatible Web3 wallet to interact with Anemoy's on-chain fund infrastructure powered by Centrifuge.
4. **Invest and manage** — Allocate capital to tokenized treasury or RWA pools and monitor your yield-bearing positions directly on-chain.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasuries** — On-chain access to U.S. Treasury exposure through tokenized fund structures designed for DeFi participants.
- **Centrifuge-Powered Infrastructure** — Built on Centrifuge's battle-tested RWA protocol, enabling transparent and compliant on-chain asset management.
- **Full Lifecycle Management** — Covers the entire process from asset origination through to investor distribution, reducing intermediary friction.
- **Institutional-Grade Credit** — Offers structured credit products meeting institutional standards while remaining accessible within DeFi ecosystems.
- **Yield-Bearing On-Chain Assets** — Investors gain exposure to real-world yield sources without leaving the blockchain environment.
- **DeFi Composability** — Tokenized fund positions can potentially integrate with broader DeFi protocols, unlocking additional utility for RWA holders.
