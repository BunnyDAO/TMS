## Getting Started

1. Visit [anemoy.io](https://www.anemoy.io) and review available tokenized asset pools.
2. Connect a compatible Web3 wallet to access on-chain fund products.
3. Complete any required KYC/AML verification for institutional-grade products.
4. Allocate to a tokenized fund — such as a Treasury bill pool — and begin earning on-chain yield.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasury Bills** — On-chain exposure to U.S. Treasury bills, providing stable, yield-bearing instruments accessible from a DeFi wallet.
- **Private Credit Access** — Institutional-grade private credit offerings brought on-chain for DeFi participants.
- **Centrifuge Infrastructure** — Built on the Centrifuge protocol, enabling transparent, auditable asset management from origination to distribution.
- **Full Lifecycle Management** — Anemoy manages assets end-to-end, from structuring and origination through to on-chain investor distribution.
- **$1B+ TVL** — One of the larger real-world asset managers in DeFi, reflecting significant institutional and on-chain investor adoption.
- **Institutional-Grade Standards** — Regulated structures designed to meet compliance requirements while remaining accessible to on-chain investors.
