## Getting Started

1. Visit [anemoy.io](https://www.anemoy.io) and review the available tokenized fund products.
2. Connect a compatible Web3 wallet to interact with Anemoy's on-chain offerings.
3. Complete any required KYC/AML verification, as institutional-grade products typically require identity checks.
4. Allocate to a fund product to gain on-chain exposure to Treasuries, credit, or other real-world assets.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasuries** — On-chain exposure to U.S. Treasury instruments wrapped in DeFi-compatible token structures.
- **Centrifuge Integration** — Built on the Centrifuge protocol, leveraging its infrastructure for real-world asset financing and distribution.
- **Full Lifecycle Management** — Covers asset origination through to investor distribution entirely on-chain.
- **Institutional-Grade Credit** — Access to credit strategies and fixed-income products typically unavailable in native DeFi.
- **DeFi Composability** — Tokenized fund positions can interact with broader DeFi ecosystems, enabling use as collateral or in liquidity strategies.
- **Real-World Yield** — Generates yield from off-chain economic activity rather than inflationary token incentives.
