## Getting Started

1. **Visit the platform** — Go to [anemoy.io](https://www.anemoy.io) to explore available tokenized asset pools and products.
2. **Check eligibility** — Anemoy targets institutional and accredited investors; review the qualification requirements for your jurisdiction.
3. **Connect via Centrifuge** — Anemoy is powered by the Centrifuge protocol, so you will interact with pools through a compatible Web3 wallet.
4. **Invest in a pool** — Select a product such as the Liquid Treasury Fund or a private credit pool and follow the onboarding steps to gain on-chain exposure.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasuries** — On-chain exposure to U.S. Treasury bills, allowing DeFi participants to hold yield-bearing government securities as tokens.
- **Private Credit Pools** — Access to institutional-grade private credit opportunities structured and managed on-chain via Centrifuge.
- **Centrifuge-Powered Infrastructure** — Built on Centrifuge's battle-tested RWA protocol, providing transparent and auditable asset management.
- **Full Lifecycle Management** — Covers the entire investment process from asset origination through to on-chain distribution and reporting.
- **DeFi Composability** — Tokenized positions can potentially be used across DeFi protocols, unlocking liquidity for traditionally illiquid assets.
- **Institutional-Grade Standards** — Applies rigorous credit underwriting and compliance standards typical of traditional asset management to on-chain products.
