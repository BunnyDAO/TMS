## Getting Started

1. **Visit Meld Gold** — Go to [meld.gold](https://meld.gold) and create an account to get started.
2. **Complete verification** — Complete any required identity verification steps to comply with regulations.
3. **Acquire Meld Tokens** — Purchase tokens where each token represents one gram of a physical precious metal.
4. **Redeem or hold** — Hold your tokens as a digital asset or redeem them for the underlying physical metal at any time.

## Key Features

- **1:1 Physical Backing** — Each Meld Token is backed by exactly one gram of a precious metal held in secure vaults.
- **Full Redeemability** — Token holders can redeem their tokens for the actual physical metal, providing a direct link to the underlying asset.
- **Secure Vault Storage** — Underlying precious metals are stored in professional, audited vaults to ensure safety and transparency.
- **Digital Liquidity** — Tokenization enables easier transfer, trading, and programmable use of gold compared to holding physical metal.
- **Transparent Ownership** — Blockchain-based tokens provide a verifiable record of ownership and provenance for each gram of metal.
