## Getting Started

1. Visit [meld.gold](https://meld.gold) and create an account to begin the onboarding process.
2. Complete any required identity verification to comply with regulatory requirements.
3. Purchase Meld tokens, where each token corresponds to one gram of a physical precious metal held in a secure vault.
4. Manage, transfer, or redeem your tokens for the underlying physical metal at any time through the platform.

## Key Features

- **Gram-denominated tokens** — Each token represents exactly one gram of a precious metal, making fractional ownership straightforward and transparent.
- **Physical backing** — All tokens are fully backed by physical precious metals stored in secure, audited vaults.
- **Full redeemability** — Token holders can redeem their tokens for the actual physical metal, unlike purely synthetic alternatives.
- **Multiple metals** — Supports gold, silver, and potentially other precious metals, giving users diversified commodity exposure.
- **Blockchain-based ownership** — Leverages blockchain technology for transparent, transferable ownership records without traditional intermediaries.
- **Secure custody** — Metals are held in professional vaulting facilities, reducing counterparty and storage risk for individual holders.
