## Getting Started

1. **Visit the platform** — Go to [app.re.xyz](https://app.re.xyz/) and connect your Web3 wallet.
2. **Review available pools** — Browse the reinsurance pools, each linked to regulated contracts with on-chain reporting.
3. **Deposit stablecoins** — Allocate capital into a pool of your choice to gain exposure to reinsurance risk and yield.
4. **Monitor performance** — Track your position and underlying contract data through the transparent on-chain dashboard.

## Key Features

- **On-chain reinsurance** — Bridges traditional reinsurance contracts onto the blockchain for transparency and accessibility.
- **Stablecoin capital pools** — Investors deposit stablecoins to participate in pooled reinsurance exposure without direct underwriting expertise.
- **Regulated contract backing** — Underlying positions are tied to regulated reinsurance agreements, providing a link to real-world legal structures.
- **Transparent reporting** — On-chain data provides visibility into capital allocation, risk exposure, and returns that is typically unavailable in traditional reinsurance.
- **Yield generation** — Participants earn yield derived from reinsurance premiums, offering an alternative to conventional DeFi or fixed-income strategies.
