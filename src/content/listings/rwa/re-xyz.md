## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.re.xyz and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Review available pools** — Browse the reinsurance capital pools and review their associated risk profiles and expected yields.
3. **Deposit stablecoins** — Provide USDC or other supported stablecoins to a pool to receive tokenized receipt assets (reUSD or reUSDe).
4. **Earn yield** — Hold your receipt tokens to accrue yield derived from regulated reinsurance contract premiums.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Reinsurance Contracts** — Real-world regulated reinsurance agreements are brought on-chain, making them transparent and auditable.
- **reUSD & reUSDe** — Receipt tokens representing depositor claims on reinsurance pool capital and accrued yield.
- **Stablecoin Yield** — Capital providers earn returns denominated in stablecoins, sourced from insurance premium income.
- **Regulated Backing** — Pools are backed by licensed reinsurance contracts, bridging DeFi capital with traditional insurance markets.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Pool composition, risk exposure, and capital flows are visible on-chain, unlike traditional reinsurance markets.
- **Ethereum Infrastructure** — Built on Ethereum, enabling composability with broader DeFi protocols and tooling.
