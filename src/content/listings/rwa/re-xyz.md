## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.re.xyz and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Deposit stablecoins** — Provide USDC or other supported stablecoins to the protocol's liquidity pools.
3. **Receive receipt tokens** — Get reUSD or reUSDe tokens representing your share of the reinsurance capital pool.
4. **Earn yield** — Accumulate returns generated from allocated real-world reinsurance contracts.

## Key Features

- **On-chain reinsurance capital** — Allocates pooled stablecoin deposits to regulated reinsurance contracts in traditional markets.
- **reUSD & reUSDe tokens** — Receipt tokens that represent depositor claims on the underlying pool and accrued yield.
- **Real-world yield source** — Returns are backed by insurance premiums rather than purely crypto-native mechanisms.
- **Tokenized risk access** — Opens a historically closed institutional market to a broader set of on-chain participants.
- **Stablecoin-denominated** — Deposits and returns are denominated in stablecoins, reducing crypto volatility exposure for participants.
