## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — DigiFT serves institutional and accredited investors; review MAS regulatory requirements and confirm you meet the onboarding criteria.
2. **Register an account** — Complete the KYC/AML process on the DigiFT platform, as required by Singapore's capital markets regulations.
3. **Access primary markets** — Participate in token issuances backed by real-world assets such as bonds, equities, or collective investment schemes.
4. **Trade on secondary markets** — Use the AMM-based trading mechanism to buy or sell asset-backed tokens with on-chain settlement.

## Key Features

- **MAS-Regulated Exchange** — Operates as a Recognised Market Operator with a Capital Markets Services license from the Monetary Authority of Singapore, providing a compliant framework for security token trading.
- **Primary Market Origination** — Enables issuers to originate and distribute tokenized real-world assets directly on-chain to eligible investors.
- **AMM-Based Liquidity** — Uses an Automated Market Maker mechanism to provide secondary market liquidity for asset-backed tokens without relying solely on order books.
- **Diverse Asset Backing** — Supports tokens backed by a range of assets including bonds, equities, digital assets, and units in collective investment schemes.
- **Public Blockchain Infrastructure** — Built on public blockchain rails, enabling transparent, auditable settlement and custody of tokenized securities.
- **Institutional Focus** — Designed for institutional and accredited investors seeking regulated on-chain exposure to real-world asset classes.
