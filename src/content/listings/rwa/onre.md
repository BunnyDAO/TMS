## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.onre.finance and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Backpack.
2. **Deposit funds** — Use the deposit interface to provide capital and receive ONyc, the reinsurance-backed stable asset.
3. **Track your position** — Monitor your ONyc balance and accruing yield directly on the OnRe dashboard.
4. **Redeem when ready** — Use the redeem function to convert ONyc back to your original asset when you wish to exit.

## Key Features

- **ONyc Stable Asset** — A Solana-native token backed by reinsurance premiums, designed to maintain stability while generating real-world yield.
- **Uncorrelated Returns** — Yield is derived from reinsurance risk rather than crypto markets, offering diversification for DeFi portfolios.
- **Institutional-Grade Yield** — Accesses the same reinsurance premium flows traditionally available only to large institutional investors.
- **Liquid and Composable** — ONyc is a standard Solana token, making it usable across the broader Solana DeFi ecosystem.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Deposits, redemptions, and yield accrual are all tracked and accessible on-chain via the dashboard.
