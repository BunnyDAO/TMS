## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit the OnRe dashboard and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Deposit funds** — Deposit supported assets to receive ONyc, a stable token backed by reinsurance premiums.
3. **Track your position** — Monitor your ONyc balance and accruing yield directly from the dashboard.
4. **Redeem anytime** — Use the redeem function to convert ONyc back to your underlying asset when ready.

## Key Features

- **ONyc Stable Asset** — A Solana-native token backed by real-world reinsurance premiums, designed to maintain stability while generating yield.
- **Uncorrelated Returns** — Yield derived from reinsurance risk has low correlation to crypto market movements, offering genuine portfolio diversification.
- **Institutional-Grade Yield** — Sourced from the global reinsurance market, historically a resilient and well-established asset class.
- **Composable DeFi Integration** — ONyc is designed to plug into Solana DeFi protocols, enabling use in lending, liquidity, and other on-chain strategies.
- **Liquid Redemptions** — Users can deposit and redeem without long lock-up periods, maintaining capital flexibility.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — All positions and balances are trackable directly on-chain via the OnRe dashboard.
