## Getting Started

1. **Verify eligibility** — VBILL is restricted to qualified institutional investors; confirm your eligibility before applying.
2. **Create a Securitize account** — Register and complete KYC/AML verification on the Securitize platform.
3. **Access the VBILL offering** — Navigate to the VanEck Treasury Fund listing on the Securitize primary market.
4. **Invest and receive tokens** — Submit your investment and receive VBILL tokens representing your proportional share of the underlying T-bill portfolio.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized T-bill exposure** — Gain on-chain access to U.S. short-term Treasury bills without holding the underlying instruments directly.
- **Daily liquidity** — The fund is structured to provide daily liquidity, unlike many traditional fixed-income products.
- **Ethereum-native tokens** — VBILL tokens are issued on Ethereum, enabling composability with broader on-chain infrastructure.
- **Institutional-grade issuer** — Backed by VanEck, a long-established asset manager with decades of experience in fixed-income and ETF products.
- **Securitize distribution** — Leverages Securitize's regulated tokenization platform for compliant issuance and investor onboarding.
- **Stable yield** — Returns are tied to short-term U.S. Treasury rates, offering a low-risk yield profile relative to crypto-native instruments.
