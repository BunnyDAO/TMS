## Getting Started

1. **Visit the platform** — Go to midas.app and review the available tokenized asset products.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Submit KYC/KYB documentation to meet regulatory compliance requirements for institutional access.
3. **Select an asset** — Choose from available tokenized treasury or credit products based on your yield and risk preferences.
4. **Integrate or hold** — Hold tokens for yield exposure or integrate them into supported DeFi protocols as collateral.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasuries** — On-chain exposure to U.S. Treasury instruments wrapped in compliant, transferable tokens.
- **Regulatory Compliance** — Built-in KYC/KYB and legal structures designed to meet institutional regulatory standards.
- **DeFi Composability** — Tokenized assets are designed to be used across DeFi protocols, including as collateral in lending markets.
- **Institutional-Grade Assets** — Access to credit and fixed-income instruments typically reserved for institutional market participants.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Asset holdings and token supply are verifiable on-chain, providing real-time auditability.
- **Multi-Asset Support** — Platform supports multiple RWA categories including treasuries and private credit instruments.
