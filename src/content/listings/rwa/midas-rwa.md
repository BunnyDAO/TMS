## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit midas.app and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Complete KYC/KYB** — Because Midas tokens are regulatory-compliant, you must pass identity verification before minting or holding tokens.
3. **Deposit collateral** — Deposit accepted stablecoins or assets to mint the corresponding RWA-backed token (e.g., mTBILL for T-bill exposure).
4. **Use in DeFi** — Deploy your minted tokens across compatible DeFi protocols to earn additional yield while maintaining underlying asset exposure.

## Key Features

- **Compliant Token Issuance** — Tokens are issued under regulatory frameworks, with on-chain allowlists ensuring only verified holders can transact.
- **Treasury-Backed Yield** — Underlying assets include short-duration U.S. Treasury bills, providing stable, low-risk yield directly on-chain.
- **DeFi Composability** — Midas tokens are ERC-20 compatible, enabling use as collateral or liquidity across DeFi protocols.
- **Institutional-Grade Custody** — Assets are held with regulated custodians, separating counterparty risk from on-chain token mechanics.
- **Transparent On-Chain Accounting** — Asset backing and NAV are verifiable on-chain, providing real-time auditability for token holders.
- **Multi-Asset Roadmap** — Beyond Treasuries, the protocol is designed to support additional real-world asset classes over time.
