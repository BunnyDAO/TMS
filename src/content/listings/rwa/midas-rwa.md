## Getting Started

1. **Visit the platform** — Go to midas.app and review the available tokenized asset products.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Undergo the KYC/KYB compliance process required to access institutional-grade tokenized assets.
3. **Deposit funds** — Connect your wallet and deposit eligible assets to mint tokenized RWA tokens.
4. **Use in DeFi** — Deploy your tokenized assets across compatible DeFi protocols for additional yield or liquidity strategies.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasuries** — Access to on-chain representations of U.S. Treasury bills and other institutional fixed-income instruments.
- **Regulatory Compliance** — Built-in KYC/KYB processes and compliance infrastructure to meet institutional and regulatory standards.
- **DeFi Composability** — Tokenized assets are designed to integrate natively with DeFi protocols, lending markets, and liquidity pools.
- **Full Transparency** — On-chain holdings and real-time data provide verifiable backing for all tokenized products.
- **Instant Liquidity** — Structured for efficient minting and redemption, reducing friction compared to traditional financial products.
- **Institutional-Grade Yield** — Exposure to yield-bearing real-world assets previously accessible only to institutional investors.
