## Getting Started

1. **Visit Midas** — Go to [midas.app](https://midas.app) and review the available tokenized asset products.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Go through the KYC/AML compliance process required for institutional access.
3. **Mint or acquire tokens** — Obtain tokenized RWA tokens representing your chosen underlying assets.
4. **Deploy in DeFi** — Use the tokenized assets across compatible DeFi protocols to earn yield or provide liquidity.

## Key Features

- **Regulatory Compliance** — Assets are tokenized within a compliance framework, including KYC/AML checks for institutional participants.
- **Institutional-Grade Assets** — Provides onchain exposure to high-quality instruments such as U.S. Treasury bills and money market assets.
- **DeFi Composability** — Tokenized RWAs can be used as collateral or within yield strategies across DeFi protocols.
- **Onchain Transparency** — Asset holdings and token supply are verifiable onchain, providing auditability for holders.
- **Tokenization Infrastructure** — Offers the underlying protocol layer for bringing a range of real-world financial assets onto public blockchains.
