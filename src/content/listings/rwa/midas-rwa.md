## Getting Started

1. **Visit the platform** — Go to [midas.app](https://midas.app) and review the available tokenized asset products.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Register and complete the required KYC/KYB compliance process to meet regulatory requirements.
3. **Connect your wallet** — Link an Ethereum-compatible wallet to interact with the protocol on-chain.
4. **Access tokenized assets** — Mint or acquire tokenized assets and deploy them across supported DeFi protocols for yield or liquidity.

## Key Features

- **Regulatory Compliance** — All tokenized products are structured to meet institutional-grade regulatory standards, enabling participation by compliant entities.
- **Tokenized Real-World Assets** — Provides on-chain exposure to assets such as U.S. Treasury bills and other institutional investment strategies.
- **DeFi Composability** — Tokenized assets are natively compatible with DeFi protocols, enabling use as collateral, liquidity, or yield-bearing instruments.
- **Full Transparency** — On-chain issuance and management provide verifiable, real-time insight into underlying asset holdings.
- **Instant Liquidity** — Unlike traditional fund structures, Midas tokens are designed to offer rapid entry and exit compared to conventional financial products.
- **Ethereum-Native** — Built on Ethereum, leveraging its security and broad ecosystem of wallets, protocols, and infrastructure.
