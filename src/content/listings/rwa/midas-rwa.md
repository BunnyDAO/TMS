## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit midas.app and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet to begin the onboarding process.
2. **Complete KYC/KYB** — Submit identity or entity verification to satisfy regulatory compliance requirements before accessing tokenized assets.
3. **Select an asset** — Choose from available tokenized products such as U.S. Treasury-backed tokens or other institutional-grade instruments.
4. **Mint or acquire tokens** — Deposit eligible stablecoins or fiat to mint RWA tokens, which can then be held, transferred, or deployed in DeFi protocols.

## Key Features

- **Regulatory Compliance** — Assets are structured to meet applicable securities regulations, enabling institutional and accredited investors to participate safely.
- **DeFi Composability** — Midas tokens are designed to integrate with lending protocols, DEXs, and yield aggregators, extending RWA utility across the DeFi ecosystem.
- **Institutional-Grade Underlying** — Tokenized exposure to high-quality assets such as U.S. Treasuries, providing stable, yield-bearing collateral on-chain.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Asset backing and token supply are verifiable on Ethereum, giving holders real-time visibility into holdings.
- **Yield Accrual** — Token holders earn yield derived from the underlying real-world assets, distributed in a compliant, on-chain manner.
- **$218M TVL** — Demonstrated adoption and liquidity signal institutional confidence in the protocol's infrastructure and compliance model.
