## Getting Started

1. **Visit the platform** — Go to [midas.app](https://midas.app) and review the available tokenized asset products.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Register and complete the required KYC/AML verification process to meet compliance requirements.
3. **Connect your wallet** — Link an Ethereum-compatible wallet to interact with tokenized asset contracts onchain.
4. **Acquire tokens** — Mint or purchase tokenized RWA products and optionally deploy them across supported DeFi protocols.

## Key Features

- **Institutional-Grade Assets** — Access tokenized real-world assets such as Treasury-backed instruments typically reserved for institutional investors.
- **Regulatory Compliance** — Built with KYC/AML requirements and regulatory considerations at the protocol level, not as an afterthought.
- **DeFi Composability** — Tokenized assets are natively composable, enabling use in lending protocols, liquidity pools, and other DeFi applications.
- **Full Transparency** — On-chain representation of underlying assets provides real-time visibility into holdings and performance.
- **Instant Liquidity** — Token structure supports faster settlement and liquidity compared to traditional fund vehicles.
- **Ethereum Native** — Deployed on Ethereum, benefiting from its security, network effects, and broad DeFi ecosystem integration.
