## Getting Started

1. **Acquire XAUt** — Purchase Tether Gold (XAUt) tokens through supported exchanges or directly via the Tether Gold platform.
2. **Set up a compatible wallet** — Use an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask to store and manage your XAUt tokens.
3. **Verify your holdings** — Each token can be verified against its corresponding physical gold bar held in Swiss vaults using the token's serial number.
4. **Redeem or trade** — Hold XAUt for on-chain gold exposure, trade it on secondary markets, or initiate a redemption for physical gold delivery.

## Key Features

- **Physical gold backing** — Each XAUt token is backed by exactly one troy fine ounce of gold stored in a secure Swiss vault.
- **On-chain ownership** — Gold ownership is represented as a blockchain token, enabling transfers without physically moving the underlying asset.
- **Swiss vault storage** — Gold is held in professional Swiss vaults, a jurisdiction known for strong financial and commodity storage standards.
- **Redeemable for physical gold** — Token holders have the ability to redeem XAUt for physical gold bars subject to eligibility requirements.
- **Ethereum-based** — XAUt operates as an ERC-20 token, making it composable with the broader DeFi ecosystem.
- **$3.2B in tokenized gold** — The largest tokenized gold product by on-chain value, offering deep liquidity relative to peers.
