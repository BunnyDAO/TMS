## Getting Started

1. **Visit MatrixDock** — Go to [matrixdock.com](https://www.matrixdock.com/home) and review eligibility requirements for accessing STBT.
2. **Complete KYC/AML** — STBT is a permissioned token, so investors must complete identity verification and onboarding through MatrixDock.
3. **Acquire STBT** — Once approved, purchase STBT tokens through the MatrixDock platform to gain exposure to U.S. Treasury bill yields.
4. **Earn Daily Yield** — Hold STBT in your wallet and receive daily rebases that reflect accrued interest from the underlying Treasury securities.

## Key Features

- **1:1 USD Peg** — STBT is designed to maintain a stable 1:1 peg to the US dollar, making it suitable as a yield-bearing stable asset.
- **Daily Rebase Yield** — Interest earned from underlying U.S. T-bills and reverse repos is rebased daily directly into holder balances.
- **Backed by T-Bills** — The underlying assets consist of short-term U.S. Treasury securities and reverse repos, providing exposure to government-backed yields.
- **On-Chain Settlement** — STBT operates on Ethereum, enabling transparent, programmable, and auditable token transfers and yield distribution.
- **RWA Reserve Layer** — MatrixDock positions STBT within a broader ecosystem supporting both permissioned and permissionless real-world asset integrations.
- **Institutional-Grade Structure** — The product is structured to meet compliance requirements, targeting institutional and accredited investors seeking on-chain Treasury exposure.
