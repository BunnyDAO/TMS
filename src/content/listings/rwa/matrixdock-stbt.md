## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — STBT is a permissioned token; visit Matrixdock's website to review KYC/AML requirements and whitelisting criteria.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Submit required identity and accreditation documents through the Matrixdock platform to become a whitelisted holder.
3. **Mint or acquire STBT** — Once approved, deposit USD to mint STBT at a 1:1 peg or acquire it through supported secondary markets.
4. **Earn yield automatically** — Hold STBT in your whitelisted wallet and receive daily rebased interest directly to your balance without any manual claiming.

## Key Features

- **1:1 USD Peg** — Each STBT is backed 1:1 by U.S. dollars and redeemable at par value, providing price stability.
- **Daily Rebase Yield** — Interest from underlying T-bills and reverse repos is automatically rebased to holders' balances each day.
- **U.S. Treasury Backing** — The underlying portfolio consists of short-term U.S. Treasury securities and reverse repurchase agreements, reflecting a risk-free rate.
- **Permissioned Access** — A whitelist-based compliance model ensures only verified participants can hold and transfer STBT.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Holdings and balances are verifiable on-chain, combining traditional fixed-income transparency with blockchain auditability.
- **RWA Reserve Layer** — Part of Matrixdock's broader ecosystem bridging traditional finance assets with DeFi and digital asset infrastructure.
