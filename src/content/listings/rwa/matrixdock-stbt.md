## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — STBT is a permissioned token; visit the Matrixdock website to verify KYC/AML requirements and supported jurisdictions.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Register and complete identity verification through the Matrixdock platform to gain access to STBT minting.
3. **Mint STBT** — Deposit USD or eligible stablecoins to mint STBT tokens backed 1:1 by U.S. Treasury bills and reverse repos.
4. **Receive daily yield** — Hold STBT in your wallet and receive daily interest rebases automatically without any manual claiming.

## Key Features

- **1:1 USD Peg** — Each STBT token is pegged to one USD, backed by short-term U.S. Treasury securities and reverse repo agreements.
- **Daily Rebase Yield** — Interest from the underlying T-bill portfolio accrues daily and is distributed via token rebase directly to holders' wallets.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Built on Ethereum, allowing holders to verify balances and transactions on-chain at any time.
- **RWA Reserve Layer** — Part of Matrixdock's ecosystem bridging traditional finance instruments with DeFi infrastructure.
- **Permissioned Access** — Designed for compliant institutional and accredited participants with KYC/AML requirements enforced at the token level.
