## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — STBT is a permissioned token; visit the Matrixdock website to complete KYC/KYB verification as required for institutional or qualified investors.
2. **Connect your wallet** — Use a compatible Ethereum wallet and connect it to the Matrixdock platform to access the minting interface.
3. **Deposit USD** — Provide USD or approved stablecoins to mint STBT tokens at a 1:1 peg to USD.
4. **Earn daily yield** — Hold STBT in your wallet and receive automatic daily rebase of interest accrued from the underlying T-bill portfolio.

## Key Features

- **Daily Yield Rebasing** — Interest from U.S. Treasury bills and reverse repos is automatically rebased to holders' wallets every day, requiring no manual claiming.
- **1:1 USD Peg** — Each STBT token is pegged to one USD, backed by short-term U.S. Treasury securities, providing capital stability alongside yield.
- **T-Bill Backed** — Underlying assets consist of short-term U.S. government Treasury securities and reverse repo agreements, minimizing credit risk.
- **On-Chain Composability** — STBT is an ERC-20 compatible token on Ethereum, enabling integration with DeFi protocols and the broader Matrixdock RWA ecosystem.
- **Permissioned Access** — Designed for verified institutional and qualified investors, with compliance built into the token's transfer mechanism.
