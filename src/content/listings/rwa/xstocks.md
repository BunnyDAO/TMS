## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit defi.xstocks.fi and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Browse available equities** — Explore the list of supported tokenized stocks available for minting or trading.
3. **Acquire xStock tokens** — Mint or purchase tokenized equity tokens representing real-world stocks.
4. **Deploy across DeFi** — Use your xStock tokens in supported lending protocols, DEXs, or other DeFi applications.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Equities** — Real-world stocks wrapped into freely transferable ERC-20 compatible on-chain tokens.
- **DeFi Composability** — xStock tokens are usable across lending platforms, DEXs, and other DeFi primitives without leaving the chain.
- **Free Transferability** — Tokens can be sent, traded, or deployed permissionlessly without intermediaries.
- **Lending Integration** — Use tokenized equities as collateral or in lending protocols to unlock liquidity.
- **DEX Compatibility** — Trade tokenized stocks directly on decentralized exchanges alongside other crypto assets.
- **On-Chain Equity Exposure** — Gain exposure to traditional equity markets through a fully on-chain, programmable interface.
