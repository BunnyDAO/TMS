## Getting Started

1. **Check eligibility** — USYC is a permissioned product intended for qualified institutional investors; verify you meet the requirements before applying.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Register through Circle or Hashnote's onboarding process, including KYC/AML verification.
3. **Acquire USYC** — Once approved, subscribe to receive USYC tokens representing your proportional share of the underlying Treasury and Reverse Repo portfolio.
4. **Deploy in DeFi** — Use USYC within approved permissioned DeFi protocols to earn yield while maintaining on-chain composability.

## Key Features

- **Short-Duration US Treasuries** — The underlying portfolio focuses on short-duration T-bills, minimizing interest rate risk while generating yield.
- **Reverse Repo Exposure** — Includes Reverse Repurchase Agreements for additional yield and capital efficiency alongside Treasury holdings.
- **Permissioned DeFi Access** — USYC can be used within vetted DeFi protocols, bridging traditional finance yields with on-chain utility.
- **Circle Integration** — Built in partnership with Circle, leveraging their stablecoin and institutional infrastructure for settlement and distribution.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Token balances and portfolio allocations are verifiable on-chain, providing real-time transparency to holders.
- **Institutional Grade** — Designed to meet the compliance and custody standards required by institutional investors accessing tokenized assets.
