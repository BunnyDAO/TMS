## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit usual.money and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Mint USD0** — Deposit supported tokenized RWA collateral or stablecoins to mint USD0, the protocol's base stablecoin.
3. **Stake for yield** — Stake USD0 to receive USD0++ or participate in yield-bearing positions distributed by the protocol.
4. **Earn USUAL tokens** — Accumulate USUAL governance tokens as rewards for providing liquidity and participating in the ecosystem.

## Key Features

- **USD0 Stablecoin** — A 1:1 USD-pegged liquid deposit token fully backed by tokenized real-world assets including T-bills.
- **Yield Redistribution** — Protocol revenue generated from RWA collateral is redistributed to liquidity providers rather than retained by the issuer.
- **USUAL Governance Token** — Holders of USUAL receive a share of protocol ownership and revenues, aligning incentives between the protocol and its users.
- **Multi-Chain Infrastructure** — Designed to operate across multiple blockchains, enabling composability with broader DeFi ecosystems.
- **Composable by Design** — USD0 is built to integrate with existing DeFi protocols, lending markets, and liquidity pools as a yield-bearing collateral asset.
