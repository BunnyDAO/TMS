## Getting Started

1. **Contact OpenTrade** — Reach out via the OpenTrade website to discuss your fintech's use case and integration requirements.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Go through the compliance and KYB process required for institutional B2B access.
3. **Integrate the API** — Connect your fintech platform to OpenTrade's infrastructure to power stablecoin yield products for your end users.
4. **Launch yield products** — Deploy compliant, T-bill-backed yield offerings to your customer base under your own brand.

## Key Features

- **White-label infrastructure** — Fintechs can offer yield products under their own brand without building the underlying on-chain stack.
- **Tokenized T-bill backing** — Yield is generated from real-world assets including U.S. Treasury bills, providing institutional-grade collateral.
- **Stablecoin-native** — Products are denominated and settled in stablecoins, enabling seamless integration with crypto-native fintech workflows.
- **Built-in compliance** — Regulatory and compliance requirements are handled at the infrastructure level, reducing burden on fintech partners.
- **B2B focus** — Designed specifically for businesses that want to embed yield products rather than individual retail investors.
- **Global reach** — Supports fintechs operating across multiple jurisdictions around the world.
