## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit hastra.io and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Explore available pools** — Review the real-world asset lending pools and their associated yield rates and risk profiles.
3. **Deposit funds** — Allocate crypto assets into your chosen pool to begin earning yields backed by real-world lending operations.
4. **Monitor performance** — Track your yield accrual and portfolio exposure through the Hastra dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Real-World Asset Backing** — Yields are derived from audited real-world lending operations, providing a tangible basis for returns rather than purely algorithmic incentives.
- **Solana Infrastructure** — Built on Solana for high throughput and low transaction costs, making participation accessible without prohibitive gas fees.
- **Figure Financial Partnership** — Developed in collaboration with Figure Financial, bringing institutional lending expertise and compliance standards to the protocol.
- **Institutional-Grade Assets** — Focuses on institutional-quality private credit opportunities typically inaccessible to retail crypto participants.
- **Sustainable Yield Model** — Designed to offer consistent, sustainable yields tied to real economic activity rather than speculative token emissions.
