## Getting Started

1. **Visit the platform** — Go to hastra.io and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Review available assets** — Browse the institutional lending pools sourced from audited real-world lending operations.
3. **Deposit funds** — Allocate capital to a yield-bearing pool to begin earning returns backed by real-world credit.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track yield accrual and pool performance directly through the Hastra dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Real-World Asset Backing** — Yields are derived from actual lending operations, not purely speculative on-chain mechanisms.
- **Solana Infrastructure** — Built on Solana for fast, low-cost transactions suitable for institutional-scale activity.
- **Figure Partnership** — Developed in collaboration with Figure, a fintech firm specializing in blockchain-based financial products.
- **Audited Lending Operations** — Underlying credit pools are audited to provide transparency and risk visibility for depositors.
- **Institutional-Grade Access** — Designed to bring institutional quality underwriting standards to DeFi participants.
- **Sustainable Yield Generation** — Focuses on durable, real-economy yield rather than token-incentivized emissions.
