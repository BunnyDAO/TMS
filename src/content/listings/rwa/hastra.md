## Getting Started

1. Visit [hastra.io](https://hastra.io/) and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. Review the available real-world asset pools and their associated yield terms sourced from Figure's lending operations.
3. Deposit supported assets into a pool to begin earning yield backed by audited private credit positions.
4. Monitor your position and accrued yield directly through the Hastra interface.

## Key Features

- **Solana-Native Protocol** — Built on Solana for fast, low-cost transactions compared to Ethereum-based RWA alternatives.
- **Figure Partnership** — Yields are sourced from Figure's audited real-world lending operations, providing a credible off-chain backing.
- **Institutional-Grade Assets** — Exposure to private credit instruments typically reserved for institutional investors.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Asset positions and yield distributions are managed and verifiable on-chain.
- **DeFi Composability** — Designed to integrate with the broader Solana DeFi ecosystem, enabling use of tokenized positions across protocols.
