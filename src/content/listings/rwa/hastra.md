## Getting Started

1. **Visit Hastra** — Go to hastra.io and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Review available assets** — Browse the real-world asset pools backed by Figure's lending operations and review yield and audit details.
3. **Deposit funds** — Allocate capital to a supported pool to begin earning yields from institutional lending activity.
4. **Monitor returns** — Track your position and accruing yield directly through the Hastra dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Real-World Asset Backing** — Yields are sourced from audited, institutional-grade lending operations rather than speculative crypto activity.
- **Figure Partnership** — Built in collaboration with Figure, a fintech company with an established track record in private credit and lending.
- **Solana Infrastructure** — Leverages Solana's high throughput and low transaction costs for efficient on-chain asset management.
- **Institutional Grade** — Designed for users seeking exposure to private credit markets with the transparency of blockchain settlement.
- **Sustainable Yields** — Returns are tied to real-world loan performance, aiming for consistent and predictable income rather than volatile DeFi incentives.
