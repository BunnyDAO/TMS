## Getting Started

1. **Visit the platform** — Go to hastra.io and explore available real-world asset pools on Solana.
2. **Connect your wallet** — Use a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare to connect to the protocol.
3. **Review lending pools** — Browse audited real-world lending opportunities and associated yield rates before depositing.
4. **Deposit and earn** — Provide liquidity to your chosen pool and begin earning yields backed by institutional-grade private credit.

## Key Features

- **Solana-native infrastructure** — Built on Solana for high throughput, low fees, and fast settlement compared to Ethereum-based RWA alternatives.
- **Figure partnership** — Developed in collaboration with Figure financial services, a established player in blockchain-based lending and financial services.
- **Real-world lending yields** — Yields are generated from audited real-world lending operations rather than token incentives or inflationary rewards.
- **Institutional-grade underwriting** — Asset selection and credit underwriting follow institutional standards to manage risk for depositors.
- **Private credit exposure** — Gives on-chain users access to private credit markets traditionally reserved for institutional investors.
