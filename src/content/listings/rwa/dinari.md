## Getting Started

1. **Visit Dinari** — Go to [dinari.com](https://dinari.com) and explore the platform or apply for API access.
2. **Integrate the API** — Use Dinari's tokenization-on-demand API to embed access to US public markets into your application.
3. **Issue or trade dShares** — Mint dShares representing real US stocks and ETFs, backed 1:1 by the underlying securities.
4. **Go live** — Deploy compliant tokenized equity products to your users globally without building custody or brokerage infrastructure from scratch.

## Key Features

- **1:1 Backed dShares** — Every dShare token is backed by a real underlying US stock or ETF held in custody, ensuring direct asset correspondence.
- **Tokenization-on-demand API** — Developers and fintechs can programmatically issue tokenized equities at scale through a straightforward API integration.
- **Broad asset coverage** — Supports a wide range of US-listed stocks and ETFs, giving global users access to American public markets.
- **Compliance-first design** — Built to work within existing legal frameworks, making it suitable for businesses that need regulatory clarity.
- **Blockchain-native** — dShares are on-chain assets that can be transferred, held, and composed within DeFi ecosystems.
- **Global accessibility** — Designed to extend US market access to users in regions that traditionally lack direct brokerage access.
