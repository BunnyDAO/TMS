## Getting Started

1. **Explore the platform** — Visit dinari.com to learn about dShares and the available tokenized equities and ETFs.
2. **Access the API** — Integrate Dinari's tokenization-on-demand API to offer US market exposure within your own application or protocol.
3. **Connect a wallet** — Link a compatible Web3 wallet to interact directly with dShare tokens on supported networks.
4. **Trade dShares** — Buy or sell tokenized US stocks and ETFs with each token fully backed 1:1 by the underlying security.

## Key Features

- **1:1 Backed dShares** — Every tokenized stock or ETF is fully backed by the real underlying asset held in custody.
- **Tokenization-on-demand API** — Developers can programmatically issue and redeem tokenized equities, enabling scalable integration into DeFi apps.
- **Global Access to US Markets** — Users outside the US can gain on-chain exposure to US public equities without traditional brokerage accounts.
- **Compliance-Friendly Design** — Built with legal and regulatory considerations in mind, making it easier for teams and their legal counsel to integrate.
- **Ethereum-Based** — dShares are deployed on Ethereum and compatible networks, enabling composability with existing DeFi protocols.
