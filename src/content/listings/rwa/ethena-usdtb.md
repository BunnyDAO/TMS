## Key Features

- **RWA-Backed** — USDtb is collateralized by tokenized real-world assets including U.S. Treasury bills, providing a stable and transparent backing.
- **USD Peg** — Designed to maintain a 1:1 peg with the US dollar, making it suitable for everyday digital transactions.
- **Payments & Trading** — Built for use in payments, trading, and as a store of value within the internet economy.
- **Ethena Ecosystem** — Issued under the Ethena protocol, benefiting from its existing infrastructure and DeFi integrations.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Collateral and reserves are represented on-chain, enabling verifiable proof of backing at any time.
