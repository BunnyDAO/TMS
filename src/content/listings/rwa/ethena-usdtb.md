1. **Visit the USDtb website** — Go to [usdtb.money](https://usdtb.money) to learn about the stablecoin and its backing.
2. **Connect a compatible wallet** — Use a Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet to interact with the protocol.
3. **Acquire USDtb** — Mint or purchase USDtb through supported platforms and Ethena's ecosystem integrations.
4. **Use in DeFi or hold** — Deploy USDtb in DeFi protocols, use it for payments, or hold it as a stable dollar-pegged asset.

## Key Features

- **BlackRock BUIDL-backed** — Primarily collateralized by BlackRock's BUIDL tokenized money market fund, one of the most credible RWA backing sources available.
- **USD Peg Stability** — Designed to maintain a 1:1 peg with the US dollar through high-quality, short-duration real-world asset collateral.
- **Issued by Ethena Labs** — Built by the team behind USDe, leveraging Ethena's established infrastructure and DeFi integrations.
- **On-chain Transparency** — Collateral and reserve backing are verifiable on-chain, providing trust and auditability.
- **DeFi Compatible** — Usable across DeFi protocols for trading, liquidity provision, and as a stable store of value.
- **Rapid Growth** — Surpassed $1B in TVL with significant week-over-week growth, reflecting strong institutional and retail adoption.
