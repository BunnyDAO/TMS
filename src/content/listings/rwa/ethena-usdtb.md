## Key Features

- **Treasury-backed collateral** — USDtb is backed by BlackRock's BUIDL fund, which holds US Treasury bills and other short-duration government securities.
- **Ethena ecosystem integration** — Issued by Ethena, USDtb integrates with the broader Ethena product suite including USDe and related infrastructure.
- **Stable dollar peg** — Designed to maintain a 1:1 peg to the US dollar through its real-world asset collateral structure.
- **On-chain transparency** — Collateral backing is verifiable on-chain through the tokenized BUIDL fund holdings.
- **Multi-purpose utility** — Suitable for payments, trading collateral, and as a stable holding asset within DeFi protocols.
- **Institutional-grade backing** — Leverages BlackRock's asset management infrastructure to bring institutional-quality collateral to on-chain stablecoins.
