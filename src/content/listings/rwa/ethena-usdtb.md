## Getting Started

1. **Connect a wallet** — Visit usdtb.money and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Mint USDtb** — Deposit accepted collateral assets to mint USDtb tokens at a 1:1 USD peg.
3. **Use USDtb in DeFi** — Deploy USDtb across supported DeFi protocols for lending, liquidity provision, or as stable collateral.
4. **Redeem** — Burn USDtb tokens at any time to reclaim the underlying collateral through the redemption process.

## Key Features

- **BlackRock BUIDL Backing** — The majority of reserves are held in BlackRock's BUIDL tokenized money market fund, providing institutional-grade collateral.
- **USD Stability** — Maintains a hard peg to the US dollar through real-world asset reserves rather than algorithmic mechanisms.
- **Over $1B TVL** — Rapid adoption with more than $1 billion in total value locked, reflecting strong market confidence.
- **Ethena Ecosystem Integration** — Works natively within the Ethena protocol alongside USDe, enabling flexible stablecoin strategies.
- **Real-World Asset Backing** — Reserves consist of short-duration U.S. Treasury instruments, minimizing counterparty and duration risk.
- **Transparent Reserves** — Backed by on-chain verifiable tokenized assets, offering greater transparency than traditional fiat-backed stablecoins.
