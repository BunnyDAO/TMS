## Key Features

- **Real-World Asset Backing** — USDtb is collateralized by real-world assets such as U.S. Treasuries, providing a transparent and familiar backing model.
- **USD Peg** — Maintains a stable 1:1 peg to the US dollar, making it suitable for everyday payments and trading use cases.
- **Separate from USDe** — Operates independently from Ethena's synthetic USDe product, offering a distinct risk profile and collateral structure.
- **Payments & Trading** — Designed for broad utility including peer-to-peer payments, on-chain trading, and as a holding asset.
- **On-Chain Dollar Liquidity** — Brings internet-native dollar liquidity to DeFi protocols and the broader blockchain ecosystem.
- **Ethena Ecosystem** — Issued under the Ethena umbrella, benefiting from the team's established infrastructure and DeFi integrations.
