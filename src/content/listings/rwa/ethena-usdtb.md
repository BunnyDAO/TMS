## Getting Started

1. **Visit the USDtb site** — Go to [usdtb.money](https://usdtb.money) to learn about the protocol and its collateral structure.
2. **Connect a wallet** — Use a compatible Ethereum wallet to interact with USDtb on supported platforms.
3. **Acquire USDtb** — Obtain USDtb through supported DeFi protocols, DEXs, or directly via the Ethena ecosystem.
4. **Monitor collateral** — Use on-chain transparency tools to verify the underlying BUIDL and treasury backing in real time.

## Key Features

- **BUIDL-backed collateral** — Primarily collateralized by BlackRock's BUIDL tokenized money market fund for institutional-grade backing.
- **On-chain transparency** — Collateral composition and reserves are verifiable directly on-chain at any time.
- **Tokenized treasury exposure** — Backed by short-duration U.S. Treasury assets via tokenized RWA infrastructure.
- **DeFi composability** — Designed to integrate with existing DeFi protocols as a stable, yield-adjacent dollar asset.
- **Ethena ecosystem integration** — Works alongside Ethena's broader product suite, including USDe, expanding stable asset options for users.
