## Getting Started

1. **Connect a wallet** — Visit usdtb.money and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Acquire USDtb** — Mint USDtb by depositing eligible collateral, or purchase it on supported decentralized exchanges.
3. **Deploy in DeFi** — Use USDtb across compatible DeFi protocols for lending, liquidity provision, or as a stable store of value.
4. **Redeem when needed** — Eligible users can redeem USDtb for underlying collateral through the Ethena platform.

## Key Features

- **BlackRock BUIDL Backing** — Primarily collateralized by BlackRock's BUIDL tokenized money market fund, providing exposure to U.S. Treasury bills on-chain.
- **Dollar Peg** — Maintains a 1:1 peg to the U.S. dollar through real-world asset collateralization rather than algorithmic methods.
- **RWA Collateralization** — Uses tokenized real-world assets as reserves, bridging traditional finance and on-chain ecosystems.
- **Ethereum Native** — Issued on Ethereum, enabling broad compatibility with the existing DeFi ecosystem and smart contract integrations.
- **Institutional Grade** — Designed with institutional-quality backing to support both retail DeFi users and larger institutional participants.
- **Ethena Ecosystem Integration** — Works alongside Ethena's other products, expanding options for users seeking stable, yield-adjacent on-chain assets.
