## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasury Backing** — Collateralized primarily by BlackRock's BUIDL fund and other tokenized real-world treasury assets for robust stability.
- **USD Peg** — Designed to maintain a 1:1 peg to the US dollar through high-quality liquid asset reserves.
- **DeFi Compatible** — Built for integration into decentralized finance protocols, trading platforms, and payment applications.
- **Issued by Ethena** — Developed by the team behind USDe, bringing established DeFi infrastructure to RWA-backed stablecoins.
- **Payments and Trading** — Optimized for use as a settlement layer in digital payments and on-chain trading environments.
- **Institutional-Grade Collateral** — Leverages BlackRock's BUIDL, the largest tokenized money market fund, as a primary backing asset.
