## Getting Started

1. **Visit the USDtb website** — Go to [usdtb.money](https://usdtb.money) to learn about the stablecoin and its backing assets.
2. **Connect a compatible wallet** — Use a Web3 wallet such as MetaMask to interact with USDtb on-chain.
3. **Acquire USDtb** — Obtain USDtb through supported exchanges, DeFi protocols, or the Ethena platform directly.
4. **Use for payments or holdings** — Hold USDtb as a stable on-chain dollar, use it for DeFi trading, or send it as a payment.

## Key Features

- **T-Bill Backing** — USDtb is collateralized by U.S. Treasury bills and other real-world assets, providing a stable and transparent foundation.
- **Digital Dollar** — Designed as a 1:1 USD-pegged stablecoin suitable for everyday on-chain use cases including payments and trading.
- **Ethena Ecosystem** — Issued by Ethena, the team behind the USDe synthetic dollar, bringing institutional-grade infrastructure to the product.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Collateral and reserves are tracked on-chain, enabling verifiable backing at any time.
- **Multi-Use** — Suitable for holding, trading on DeFi platforms, or transferring value across the internet economy.
- **RWA Integration** — Bridges traditional finance instruments like T-bills with decentralized finance through tokenization.
