## Key Features

- **BUIDL-backed reserves** — Stablecoin reserves are held in BlackRock's BUIDL tokenized U.S. Treasury fund, providing institutional-grade backing.
- **USD peg** — USDtb maintains a 1:1 peg to the US dollar, designed for stability in volatile market conditions.
- **On-chain transparency** — Reserve composition and collateral data are verifiable on-chain, supporting auditability.
- **DeFi compatible** — Designed as a composable digital dollar usable across Ethereum-based protocols, DEXs, and lending markets.
- **Payments and trading** — Suited for a range of use cases including cross-border payments, on-chain trading, and stable value storage.
- **Ethena ecosystem integration** — Complements Ethena's broader product suite, offering an alternative stable asset with a conservative treasury-backed model.
