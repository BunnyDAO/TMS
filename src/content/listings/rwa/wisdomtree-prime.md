## Getting Started

1. **Create an account** — Visit WisdomTree Prime and sign up, completing the required identity verification process.
2. **Fund your account** — Deposit funds via supported methods to begin purchasing tokenized assets.
3. **Explore digital assets** — Browse available tokenized funds, U.S. Dollar tokens, Gold tokens, and crypto offerings.
4. **Invest and manage** — Allocate to tokenized funds or digital assets and monitor your holdings through the app dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Funds** — Access digitally-native investment funds backed by real-world assets such as U.S. Treasuries, issued on blockchain infrastructure.
- **Gold and Dollar Tokens** — Hold and transfer tokenized representations of gold and U.S. dollars directly within the app.
- **Retail and Institutional Access** — Platform is designed to serve both individual retail users and institutional investors under a regulated framework.
- **Spend and Transfer** — Use digital asset balances for spending and peer-to-peer transfers, going beyond traditional investment-only platforms.
- **Regulated Asset Manager** — Backed by WisdomTree, a publicly traded and SEC-registered asset manager with decades of experience in ETFs and funds.
- **Blockchain Infrastructure** — Assets are issued on-chain, enabling transparency, programmability, and interoperability with broader DeFi and TradFi ecosystems.
