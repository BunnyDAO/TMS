## Getting Started

1. **Create an account** — Sign up on the WisdomTree Prime app and complete identity verification to access digital asset products.
2. **Fund your account** — Deposit funds via supported methods to begin purchasing tokenized assets or digital funds.
3. **Browse available assets** — Explore offerings including U.S. Dollar tokens, Gold tokens, crypto, and tokenized digital funds.
4. **Invest and manage** — Buy, hold, transfer, or spend your digital assets directly within the WisdomTree Prime platform.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Real-World Assets** — Access blockchain-based representations of traditional assets like U.S. Dollars and Gold, issued by a regulated asset manager.
- **Digital Funds** — Invest in tokenized fund products that mirror conventional fund structures but settle on-chain.
- **WisdomTree Connect** — The underlying infrastructure layer enabling institutions to access and integrate WisdomTree's tokenized asset offerings.
- **Spend and Transfer** — Use digital asset balances for transfers and spending, not just passive investment.
- **Regulated Issuer** — WisdomTree is an established asset manager, providing a level of regulatory credibility uncommon in the tokenization space.
- **Multi-Asset Support** — Combines crypto, tokenized commodities, dollar-pegged tokens, and digital funds in a single app.
