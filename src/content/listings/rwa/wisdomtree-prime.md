## Getting Started

1. **Visit WisdomTree Prime** — Go to wisdomtreeprime.com and create an account to access the platform's digital asset offerings.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Finish identity verification and any institutional eligibility requirements to unlock the full suite of products.
3. **Explore available assets** — Browse tokenized funds, U.S. Dollar tokens, Gold tokens, and supported cryptocurrencies available on-chain.
4. **Save, spend, or invest** — Use the platform to hold digital assets, transfer value, or invest in tokenized fund products suited to your profile.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Funds** — Access WisdomTree's regulated investment funds represented as blockchain tokens, combining traditional finance structures with on-chain settlement.
- **Digital Dollar & Gold Tokens** — Hold and transfer U.S. Dollar and Gold-backed tokens directly within the app for everyday financial use.
- **WisdomTree Connect** — An institutional-grade API and infrastructure layer enabling traditional and crypto-native financial firms to integrate tokenized RWAs.
- **Multi-Asset Support** — The platform supports a range of assets including crypto, digital funds, and commodity-backed tokens under one interface.
- **Blockchain-Powered Settlement** — Transactions and holdings are recorded on blockchain infrastructure, enabling transparency and programmability.
- **Regulated Issuer** — Products are issued by WisdomTree, an established asset manager with a long track record in regulated financial products.
