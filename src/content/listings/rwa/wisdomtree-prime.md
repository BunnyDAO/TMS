## Getting Started

1. **Visit WisdomTree Prime** — Go to wisdomtreeprime.com and create an account to access the platform.
2. **Complete onboarding** — Verify your identity and connect your financial accounts or wallets as required.
3. **Explore digital assets** — Browse available tokenized assets including U.S. Dollar tokens, Gold tokens, and digital funds.
4. **Invest or transfer** — Use the app to save, spend, transfer, or invest in tokenized real-world assets on-chain.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Funds** — Access WisdomTree's existing fund lineup in tokenized, blockchain-native form for seamless on-chain exposure.
- **U.S. Dollar & Gold Tokens** — Hold and transact with digital tokens backed by real-world dollar and gold instruments.
- **WisdomTree Connect** — Institutional API layer enabling traditional and crypto-native financial firms to integrate RWA access.
- **Multi-Asset Coverage** — Supports a range of digital assets including crypto, tokenized funds, and commodity-backed tokens in one app.
- **TradFi Bridge** — Built by an established asset manager, providing regulatory familiarity and institutional-grade credibility for on-chain finance.
- **Spend & Transfer** — Beyond investing, the platform supports everyday financial actions like spending and transferring digital assets.
