## Getting Started

1. Visit [WisdomTree Prime](https://www.wisdomtreeprime.com/) and create an account.
2. Complete identity verification as required for your account type (retail or institutional).
3. Explore available digital assets including tokenized funds, U.S. Dollar tokens, and Gold tokens.
4. Begin saving, spending, transferring, or investing in your chosen digital assets via the platform.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Funds** — Access blockchain-based representations of traditional investment funds directly on-chain.
- **Digital Dollar & Gold Tokens** — Hold and transact U.S. Dollar and Gold tokens as digital assets.
- **WisdomTree Connect** — Institutional-grade platform enabling banks and asset managers to access tokenized RWAs.
- **Multi-Asset Support** — Manage crypto, digital funds, and tokenized real-world assets from a single interface.
- **Blockchain-Powered Settlement** — Leverages blockchain technology to streamline fund access and settlement processes.
- **TradFi & DeFi Bridge** — Designed to serve both traditional financial institutions and crypto-native organizations.
