## Getting Started

1. **Visit WisdomTree Prime** — Go to wisdomtreeprime.com and create an account to access the digital asset app or explore institutional offerings via WisdomTree Connect.
2. **Complete verification** — Go through the required identity and compliance verification, as WisdomTree operates as a regulated financial services provider.
3. **Choose your product** — Select from tokenized assets such as U.S. Dollar tokens, Gold tokens, digital funds, or crypto depending on your investor profile.
4. **Save, spend, or invest** — Use the platform to hold, transfer, or invest in digital assets backed by regulated fund infrastructure.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Real-World Assets** — Access blockchain-based representations of traditional assets including U.S. Dollar and Gold tokens issued under regulated fund structures.
- **WisdomTree Connect** — Institutional gateway offering crypto-native and traditional finance institutions seamless access to tokenized investment products.
- **Regulated Infrastructure** — Products are built within existing investment fund regulatory frameworks, providing compliance assurance not found in many crypto-native alternatives.
- **Digital Funds** — Invest in tokenized fund products that combine the familiarity of traditional asset management with on-chain settlement and transparency.
- **Multi-Asset App** — Consumer-facing app supporting saving, spending, transferring, and investing across digital assets and crypto in one interface.
- **TradFi-Crypto Bridge** — Designed to serve both institutional players from traditional finance and crypto-native organizations seeking regulated on-chain exposure.
