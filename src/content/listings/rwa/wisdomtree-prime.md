## Getting Started

1. **Download the app** — WisdomTree Prime is available as a mobile app; sign up and complete identity verification as required by the platform.
2. **Fund your account** — Deposit USD or connect an existing account to begin accessing digital asset products.
3. **Explore tokenized assets** — Browse available products including tokenized Treasury-backed funds, gold tokens, and USD tokens.
4. **Save, spend, or invest** — Use the platform's features to manage your digital asset holdings, transfer funds, or invest in tokenized funds.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Treasury Funds** — Access blockchain-based funds backed by U.S. Treasury assets, combining traditional finance stability with on-chain accessibility.
- **Gold and USD Tokens** — Hold tokenized representations of gold and U.S. dollars directly within the app.
- **Regulated Issuer** — WisdomTree is an established, SEC-registered asset manager, providing a layer of regulatory oversight uncommon in the tokenized asset space.
- **Multi-Asset Platform** — Manage crypto, digital funds, and tokenized real-world assets in a single application.
- **Spend and Transfer** — Beyond investing, the platform supports spending and transferring digital assets, bridging traditional and on-chain finance.
