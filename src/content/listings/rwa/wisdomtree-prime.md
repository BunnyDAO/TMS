## Getting Started

1. **Create an account** — Sign up on the WisdomTree Prime app or web platform to access digital asset products.
2. **Fund your wallet** — Deposit funds to acquire digital assets such as U.S. Dollar tokens or Gold tokens.
3. **Explore investment options** — Browse available tokenized funds, crypto assets, and digital commodities.
4. **Save, spend, or transfer** — Use your digital assets for everyday financial activities or long-term investment purposes.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized U.S. Dollar & Gold** — Hold blockchain-native representations of traditional assets directly in the app.
- **Digital Funds** — Access WisdomTree's tokenized fund products on-chain, bridging conventional asset management with blockchain rails.
- **WisdomTree Connect** — Institutional-grade infrastructure allowing traditional and crypto-native financial firms to integrate tokenized RWAs.
- **Multi-asset platform** — Manage crypto, digital commodities, and tokenized funds from a single unified interface.
- **Regulated issuer** — Backed by WisdomTree, an established global asset manager with decades of ETF and fund management experience.
- **Blockchain infrastructure** — Assets issued and settled on public blockchain networks, enabling transparency and programmability.
