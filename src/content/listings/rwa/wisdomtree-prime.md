## Getting Started

1. Visit [WisdomTree Prime](https://www.wisdomtreeprime.com/) and create an account to access the consumer app or explore institutional offerings.
2. Complete identity verification as required by the platform's regulatory compliance process.
3. Browse available digital assets including U.S. Dollar tokens, Gold tokens, crypto, and digital funds.
4. Save, spend, transfer, or invest in your chosen assets directly through the app or institutional integration layer.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Real-World Assets** — Access digitized versions of traditional financial products including U.S. Dollar and Gold tokens backed by regulated instruments.
- **Digital Funds** — Invest in WisdomTree's proprietary on-chain fund products combining familiar fund structures with blockchain settlement.
- **WisdomTree Connect** — An institutional-grade integration platform allowing traditional and crypto-native firms to access tokenized RWAs programmatically.
- **Regulated Issuer** — Offered by WisdomTree, an established and regulated asset manager with decades of experience in ETFs and financial products.
- **Multi-Asset Support** — Combines crypto assets, tokenized commodities, and digital cash instruments in a single unified platform.
- **Blockchain-Native Settlement** — Leverages distributed ledger technology for transparent, efficient transfer and custody of digital assets.
