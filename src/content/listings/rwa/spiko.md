## Getting Started

1. **Create an account** — Visit spiko.io and open your account in under 5 minutes by completing the onboarding and KYC process.
2. **Choose your fund** — Select USTBL for exposure to US Treasury Bills or EUTBL for Eurozone Treasury Bills.
3. **Deposit funds** — Transfer cash into your chosen money market fund to begin earning daily interest.
4. **Withdraw anytime** — Redeem your position at any time with no notice period, fees, or penalties applied.

## Key Features

- **Regulated Funds** — Both USTBL and EUTBL are regulated by the French Financial Markets Authority (AMF), providing strong investor protections.
- **Daily Interest** — Interest accrues and is paid daily, offering transparent and consistent yield on held cash.
- **Two Fund Options** — USTBL targets US T-Bills while EUTBL targets Eurozone T-Bills, giving users geographic diversification.
- **No Lock-up** — Funds can be withdrawn at any time without notice requirements, fees, or early exit penalties.
- **Tokenized Structure** — Fund shares are represented on-chain, enabling the transparency and accessibility benefits of blockchain-based finance.
- **Fast Onboarding** — Account setup takes less than 5 minutes, lowering the barrier to accessing institutional-grade treasury products.
