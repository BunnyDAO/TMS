## Getting Started

1. **Create an account** — Visit spiko.io and open your account in under 5 minutes by completing the onboarding process.
2. **Complete verification** — Pass the required KYC/AML checks as mandated by the AMF regulatory framework.
3. **Choose your fund** — Select USTBL for exposure to US Treasury Bills or EUTBL for Eurozone T-Bills.
4. **Deposit and earn** — Fund your account and begin receiving daily interest with the ability to withdraw at any time without notice or penalties.

## Key Features

- **AMF-Regulated Funds** — Both USTBL and EUTBL are overseen by the French Financial Markets Authority, providing institutional-grade regulatory compliance.
- **Daily Interest Payments** — Interest accrues and is paid daily, giving users continuous yield on their cash holdings.
- **No Lock-Up or Fees** — Funds can be withdrawn at any time with no notice period, redemption fees, or exit penalties.
- **Dual Currency Exposure** — USTBL targets US T-Bills while EUTBL targets Eurozone T-Bills, offering diversified short-duration sovereign debt exposure.
- **On-Chain Settlement** — Funds are tokenized on Ethereum, enabling transparent and programmable ownership of regulated money market instruments.
- **Fast Onboarding** — Retail and institutional users can open an account in less than 5 minutes with a streamlined digital process.
