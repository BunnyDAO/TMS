## Getting Started

1. **Open an account** — Visit spiko.io and complete the onboarding process in under 5 minutes.
2. **Complete verification** — Submit required KYC documentation to meet regulatory requirements set by the AMF.
3. **Deposit funds** — Transfer cash into your Spiko account to begin earning interest.
4. **Earn daily interest** — Your holdings in USTBL or EUTBL accrue interest daily, and you can withdraw at any time with no notice or penalties.

## Key Features

- **AMF-Regulated Funds** — Spiko's money market funds are fully regulated by the French Financial Markets Authority, providing a compliant on-chain investment vehicle.
- **US T-Bills (USTBL)** — Gain tokenized exposure to short-term US Treasury bills for dollar-denominated yield.
- **Eurozone T-Bills (EUTBL)** — Access euro-denominated tokenized treasury exposure through Eurozone T-Bills.
- **Daily Interest Payments** — Interest accrues and is paid daily, offering transparent and frequent returns.
- **No Lock-Up or Fees** — Withdraw funds at any time with no notice period, exit fees, or penalties.
- **Fast Onboarding** — Account setup takes less than 5 minutes, lowering the barrier to accessing regulated treasury yield.
