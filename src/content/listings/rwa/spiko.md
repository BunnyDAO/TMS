## Getting Started

1. **Create an account** — Open your Spiko account in less than 5 minutes directly on the platform.
2. **Complete verification** — Submit the required KYC documents to comply with AMF regulatory requirements.
3. **Deposit funds** — Transfer your cash into the money market fund of your choice (USTBL or EUTBL).
4. **Earn daily interest** — Interest accrues daily and can be withdrawn at any time with no lock-up or fees.

## Key Features

- **AMF Regulated** — Fully regulated by the French Financial Markets Authority, providing institutional-grade compliance and investor protection.
- **US T-Bills (USTBL)** — Tokenized exposure to short-term US Treasury bills, offering stable dollar-denominated yield on-chain.
- **Eurozone T-Bills (EUTBL)** — Tokenized exposure to Eurozone T-Bills for euro-denominated cash management.
- **Daily Interest Payments** — Interest is calculated and credited daily, with no waiting period for accrual.
- **Instant Liquidity** — Withdraw your funds at any time with no notice requirements, redemption fees, or penalties.
- **$1.24B TVL** — One of Europe's largest regulated tokenized treasury platforms by total value locked.
