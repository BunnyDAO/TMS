## Key Features

- **AMF-Regulated Funds** — Spiko's money market funds are formally regulated by the French Financial Markets Authority, providing a strong compliance framework.
- **US T-Bill Exposure (USTBL)** — Gain tokenized exposure to US Treasury Bills for USD-denominated yield.
- **Eurozone T-Bill Exposure (EUTBL)** — Access tokenized Eurozone Treasury Bills for EUR-denominated yield.
- **Daily Interest Payments** — Interest accrues and is paid daily, unlike traditional fund structures with longer settlement cycles.
- **No Lock-Up** — Withdraw your capital at any time without penalties, notice periods, or withdrawal fees.
- **Fast Onboarding** — Account setup is designed to be completed in less than 5 minutes.
