## Getting Started

1. **Open an account** — Visit spiko.io and complete the onboarding process in under 5 minutes.
2. **Complete verification** — Pass the required KYC/AML checks to comply with AMF regulatory requirements.
3. **Fund your account** — Deposit cash to subscribe to either the USTBL (US T-Bills) or EUTBL (Eurozone T-Bills) fund.
4. **Earn daily interest** — Interest accrues daily and you can withdraw at any time with no notice period or penalties.

## Key Features

- **AMF Regulation** — Fully regulated by the French Financial Markets Authority, providing institutional-grade compliance and investor protection.
- **Dual Fund Options** — Choose between USTBL for exposure to US Treasury Bills or EUTBL for Eurozone Treasury Bills.
- **Daily Interest Payments** — Interest is paid daily, offering transparent and predictable yield on idle cash.
- **No Lock-up or Fees** — Withdraw at any time with no notice requirements, exit fees, or penalties.
- **Tokenized Structure** — Fund shares are represented on-chain, combining traditional finance compliance with blockchain accessibility.
- **Significant TVL** — Over $1.2B in total value locked, reflecting strong institutional and retail adoption.
