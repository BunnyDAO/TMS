## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.re.xyz and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Deposit stablecoins** — Supply supported stablecoins to the Re Protocol liquidity pool.
3. **Receive tokenized receipts** — Get reUSD or reUSDe tokens representing your proportional share of the pool.
4. **Earn yield** — Your capital is allocated to regulated reinsurance contracts, generating yield from real-world insurance premiums.

## Key Features

- **Tokenized Reinsurance Exposure** — Access the global reinsurance market through on-chain tokenized positions (reUSD, reUSDe) without traditional intermediaries.
- **Stablecoin Deposits** — Capital is supplied and denominated in stablecoins, reducing crypto volatility risk for yield-seekers.
- **Real-World Asset Backing** — Funds are deployed into regulated reinsurance contracts, linking on-chain capital to real insurance risk and premiums.
- **On-Chain Transparency** — Pool allocations and capital flows are visible on Ethereum, making a historically opaque market auditable.
- **Yield Generation** — Depositors earn returns derived from reinsurance premiums rather than speculative DeFi incentives.
