## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [kamino.finance](https://kamino.finance/) and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Backpack.
2. **Deposit assets** — Choose a supported token market and deposit assets to start earning yield from borrowers.
3. **Borrow against collateral** — Use your deposited assets as collateral to borrow other tokens against them at transparent onchain rates.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor, interest accrued, and collateral ratio directly in the dashboard to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Peer-to-pool lending** — Assets are pooled together so lenders earn continuous yield while borrowers access liquidity without a counterparty match.
- **Institutional-grade infrastructure** — Designed as a composable primitive that other DeFi protocols and financial products can build on top of.
- **Transparent onchain markets** — All interest rates, collateral ratios, and liquidation parameters are determined and enforced onchain.
- **Multi-asset collateral** — Supports a wide range of Solana tokens as collateral, enabling flexible borrowing strategies.
- **Open source protocol** — The codebase is publicly available, allowing developers to audit, integrate, and build on Kamino Lend.
- **High liquidity** — Over $1.67B TVL provides deep markets with competitive rates for both lenders and borrowers.
