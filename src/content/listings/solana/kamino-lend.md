## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [kamino.finance](https://kamino.finance/) and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Backpack.
2. **Supply assets** — Deposit supported tokens into a lending market to start earning yield from borrower interest.
3. **Borrow against collateral** — Use your supplied assets as collateral to borrow other tokens, managing your loan-to-value ratio to avoid liquidation.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor, accrued interest, and available liquidity directly in the dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Peer-to-pool lending** — Liquidity is pooled from suppliers and made available to borrowers, enabling efficient capital deployment without order matching.
- **Leverage support** — Protocol is architected to support leveraged positions and looping strategies commonly used in advanced DeFi.
- **Automation-ready** — Designed as infrastructure for automated financial products, making it composable with other Solana protocols.
- **Transparent onchain markets** — All positions, rates, and risk parameters are fully verifiable on the Solana blockchain.
- **Multi-asset markets** — Supports a range of Solana-native tokens as collateral and borrowable assets.
- **Open source** — Smart contract code is publicly available, allowing independent security review and third-party integrations.
