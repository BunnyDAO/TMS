## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [kamino.finance](https://kamino.finance/) and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Deposit collateral** — Choose an asset to supply to the lending pool and start earning yield from borrower interest.
3. **Borrow against collateral** — Once you have supplied assets, you can borrow other supported tokens up to your available credit limit.
4. **Monitor your health factor** — Keep track of your loan-to-value ratio to avoid liquidation as market prices fluctuate.

## Key Features

- **Peer-to-pool lending** — Assets are pooled together so lenders earn continuously from borrower demand without needing to match individual counterparties.
- **Leverage support** — Protocol architecture is designed to support leveraged positions and automated strategies built on top of the core lending markets.
- **Transparent onchain markets** — All interest rates, collateral factors, and risk parameters are managed onchain and publicly auditable.
- **Multi-asset collateral** — Supports a range of blue-chip Solana assets as collateral, giving users flexibility in how they structure positions.
- **Composable infrastructure** — Designed as a building block for other DeFi protocols and applications requiring credit or liquidity primitives.
- **Institutional and retail access** — Open to both retail users and institutions seeking programmatic access to Solana credit markets.
