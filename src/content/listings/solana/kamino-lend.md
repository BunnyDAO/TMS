## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [kamino.finance](https://kamino.finance) and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Backpack.
2. **Deposit collateral** — Choose an asset to supply to one of Kamino's onchain lending markets to start earning yield.
3. **Borrow against collateral** — Use your deposited assets as collateral to borrow supported tokens based on available loan-to-value ratios.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and manage risk to avoid liquidation as market conditions change.

## Key Features

- **Peer-to-Pool Lending** — Assets are pooled together, allowing borrowers to access liquidity and lenders to earn interest without requiring a counterpart match.
- **Leverage Support** — Designed as foundational infrastructure for leverage products, enabling users to amplify exposure to Solana-native assets.
- **Automated Strategies** — Integrates with automation tooling to support looping, yield strategies, and programmatic position management.
- **Transparent Onchain Markets** — All positions, rates, and collateral are verifiable on-chain, providing full auditability.
- **Institutional and Retail Access** — Protocol is open to both individual users and institutions seeking credit and liquidity solutions.
- **High TVL and Liquidity** — Over $1.6B in TVL with consistent week-over-week growth, indicating deep liquidity across markets.
