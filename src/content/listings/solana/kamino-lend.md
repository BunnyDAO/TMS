## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit kamino.finance and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Backpack.
2. **Deposit collateral** — Supply supported assets to a lending market to start earning yield or to use as collateral for borrowing.
3. **Borrow against collateral** — Select an asset to borrow against your deposited collateral, keeping an eye on your loan-to-value ratio.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and adjust collateral or repay loans as needed to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Peer-to-pool lending** — Deposit assets into shared pools and earn interest from borrowers without needing a counterparty match.
- **Collateralized borrowing** — Borrow against a wide range of Solana-native assets using transparent, onchain loan parameters.
- **Leverage support** — Built specifically to serve as infrastructure for leveraged strategies and automated DeFi products.
- **Institutional and retail access** — Designed to serve both sophisticated institutions and everyday DeFi users through the same protocol.
- **Onchain transparency** — All market parameters, interest rates, and positions are fully visible and verifiable on Solana.
- **Open source** — Protocol code is publicly available, enabling integrations and composability with other Solana DeFi applications.
