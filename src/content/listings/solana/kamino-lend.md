## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [kamino.finance](https://kamino.finance/) and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Supply assets** — Deposit supported tokens into Kamino Lend markets to earn interest and make them available as collateral.
3. **Borrow against collateral** — Once you have supplied assets, borrow other tokens against your deposited collateral within the protocol's risk parameters.
4. **Monitor your position** — Keep track of your health factor and loan-to-value ratio to avoid liquidation as market prices move.

## Key Features

- **Peer-to-Pool Model** — Liquidity is pooled so borrowers and lenders interact with shared markets rather than directly with each other, improving capital efficiency.
- **Leveraged Strategies** — Designed as infrastructure for automated leverage loops and other advanced DeFi strategies built on top of the lending layer.
- **Onchain Transparency** — All markets, rates, and positions are fully transparent and verifiable on the Solana blockchain.
- **Institutional and Retail Access** — Supports both retail users and institutions looking to generate yield or access liquidity against crypto holdings.
- **Composable Infrastructure** — Built to integrate with other Solana DeFi protocols, enabling developers to build automated financial products on top of K-Lend.
