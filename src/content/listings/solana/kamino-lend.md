## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit kamino.finance and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Backpack.
2. **Supply assets** — Deposit supported tokens into Kamino's lending pools to start earning yield from borrower interest.
3. **Borrow against collateral** — Use your supplied assets as collateral to borrow other tokens, enabling leverage or liquidity without selling your holdings.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and loan-to-value ratio in the dashboard to manage liquidation risk.

## Key Features

- **Peer-to-pool lending** — Assets are pooled together so lenders earn continuous interest from borrowers without needing matched counterparts.
- **Collateralized borrowing** — Borrow against a range of crypto assets with transparent on-chain collateral ratios and liquidation parameters.
- **Leverage support** — The protocol is architected to serve as infrastructure for leveraged strategies and automated DeFi products built on top.
- **Institutional and retail access** — Open to both retail users and institutions through the same transparent, permissionless onchain markets.
- **Solana-native performance** — Benefits from Solana's high throughput and low transaction costs, making lending and borrowing economically viable at any scale.
- **Open source** — Protocol code is publicly available, enabling community auditing and composability with other Solana DeFi applications.
