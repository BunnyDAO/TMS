## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit kamino.finance and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Supply assets** — Deposit supported tokens into a lending market to start earning yield on your holdings.
3. **Borrow against collateral** — Use your supplied assets as collateral to borrow other tokens for leverage or liquidity needs.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your loan-to-value ratio and health factor to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Peer-to-Pool Lending** — Assets are pooled together so lenders earn yield and borrowers access liquidity without needing a direct counterparty.
- **Leverage Support** — Protocol architecture is designed to support leveraged positions and automated DeFi strategies.
- **DeFi Composability** — Built as foundational infrastructure that other Solana protocols can integrate and build upon.
- **Onchain Transparency** — All markets operate transparently on Solana with publicly verifiable smart contract logic.
- **Institutional & Retail Access** — Designed to serve both individual users and institutions seeking onchain credit markets.
- **High TVL** — Over $1.6 billion in total value locked, reflecting significant adoption across the Solana ecosystem.
