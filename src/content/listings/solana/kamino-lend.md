## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit kamino.finance and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Backpack.
2. **Deposit collateral** — Supply supported assets to a Kamino lending market to earn yield and unlock borrowing power.
3. **Borrow assets** — Use your deposited collateral to borrow tokens at market-determined interest rates.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your loan-to-value ratio and health factor to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Peer-to-Pool Lending** — Deposits are pooled and matched with borrowers algorithmically, removing the need for direct counterparty matching.
- **Leverage Support** — The protocol is architected to serve as infrastructure for leveraged strategies and automated financial products.
- **Onchain Transparency** — All markets, rates, and positions are fully visible and verifiable on the Solana blockchain.
- **Institutional and Retail Access** — Designed to serve both sophisticated institutions and everyday DeFi users from a single protocol.
- **Composability** — Built to integrate with other Solana protocols, enabling developers to build complex financial products on top of Kamino's credit layer.
- **Automated Risk Management** — Liquidation mechanisms and health factor monitoring help protect the protocol and its users from undercollateralized positions.
