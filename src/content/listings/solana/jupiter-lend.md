## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [jup.ag](https://jup.ag) and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Navigate to Lend** — Select the Lend tab from the Jupiter navigation bar to view available markets and current interest rates.
3. **Supply or borrow** — Deposit supported tokens to earn yield, or provide collateral to open a borrow position against your assets.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your supplied assets, borrow utilization, and health factor directly from the dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Integrated with Jupiter DEX** — Lending is natively embedded in Jupiter's broader DeFi suite alongside swaps, perpetuals, and a wallet, reducing the need to switch platforms.
- **$1.2B+ TVL** — One of the largest lending protocols on Solana, reflecting deep liquidity across supported asset markets.
- **Earn yield on deposits** — Supply tokens to lending pools and earn variable interest rates driven by real borrow demand.
- **Collateralized borrowing** — Borrow against deposited assets while maintaining a health factor to avoid liquidation.
- **Wide asset support** — Access lending markets for major Solana-native and bridged tokens within a single interface.
- **Non-custodial** — Funds are managed by on-chain smart contracts; users retain control via their own wallets at all times.
