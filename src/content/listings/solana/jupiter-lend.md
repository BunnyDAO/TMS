## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [jup.ag](https://jup.ag) and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Choose your action** — Navigate to the Lend section and decide whether you want to supply assets to earn interest or borrow against deposited collateral.
3. **Deposit collateral or assets** — Select a supported token, enter an amount, and confirm the transaction to begin earning yield or unlock borrowing capacity.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your supplied assets, borrow utilization, and health factor directly in the Jupiter dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Peer-to-Pool Lending** — Assets are pooled together, allowing lenders to earn yield while borrowers draw from shared liquidity without needing a counterparty match.
- **$1B+ TVL** — One of Solana's largest lending protocols by total value locked, reflecting strong ecosystem trust and adoption.
- **Jupiter Ecosystem Integration** — Seamlessly connected to Jupiter's swap aggregator, perpetuals, and wallet, enabling a unified DeFi experience in one interface.
- **Liquid Staking Support** — Accepts liquid staking tokens as collateral, letting users put staked assets to work while retaining yield exposure.
- **Transparent Risk Parameters** — Loan-to-value ratios, liquidation thresholds, and interest rate models are visible on-chain for full auditability.
- **No Protocol Fees to Start** — Designed to be accessible with competitive rates driven by supply and demand within each asset pool.
