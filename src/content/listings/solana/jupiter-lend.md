## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [jup.ag](https://jup.ag/) and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Navigate to Lend** — Select the Lend section from the Jupiter navigation menu to access the lending and borrowing interface.
3. **Supply assets** — Choose a supported token to deposit as collateral or to supply as liquidity and start earning yield.
4. **Borrow against collateral** — Once you have supplied collateral, select an asset to borrow against it, keeping an eye on your health factor to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Lending & Yield** — Supply supported Solana tokens to liquidity pools and earn interest from borrowers.
- **Collateralized Borrowing** — Borrow assets against deposited collateral with transparent loan-to-value ratios.
- **Jupiter Ecosystem Integration** — Seamlessly connected with Jupiter Swap, Perps, and wallet tools for an all-in-one DeFi experience.
- **Solana-Native Speed** — Benefits from Solana's fast block times and low transaction fees for efficient money-market interactions.
- **Health Factor Monitoring** — Real-time visibility into collateral health to help users manage risk and avoid liquidation.
