## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit swap.pump.fun and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Select tokens** — Choose the input and output tokens you want to swap, including newly launched pump.fun assets.
3. **Review the swap** — Check the exchange rate, price impact, and estimated fees before confirming.
4. **Confirm the transaction** — Approve the transaction in your wallet and wait for on-chain confirmation.
