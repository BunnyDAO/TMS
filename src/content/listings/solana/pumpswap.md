## Getting Started

1. **Connect a wallet** — Visit swap.pump.fun and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Select tokens** — Choose the token pair you want to swap, including any meme token launched via pump.fun.
3. **Enter an amount** — Input the amount you wish to trade and review the quoted output and price impact.
4. **Confirm the swap** — Approve the transaction in your wallet to execute the trade on-chain.

## Key Features

- **pump.fun Integration** — Directly connected to the pump.fun launchpad, providing liquidity for tokens after they graduate from the bonding curve.
- **AMM Model** — Uses an automated market maker design to enable permissionless, on-chain token swaps without an order book.
- **High TVL** — Holds over $257M in total value locked, reflecting significant liquidity depth for meme token trading.
- **Solana Speed** — Benefits from Solana's fast block times and low transaction fees for efficient trade execution.
- **Permissionless Trading** — Any token launched on pump.fun can be traded without manual listing or approval processes.
