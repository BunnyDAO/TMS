## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [swap.pump.fun](https://swap.pump.fun) and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Select tokens** — Choose the token pair you want to swap, including any meme or community tokens launched on pump.fun.
3. **Review the trade** — Check the exchange rate, price impact, and any applicable fees before confirming.
4. **Confirm the swap** — Approve the transaction in your wallet to execute the trade on-chain.

- **Native pump.fun integration** — Directly connected to the pump.fun ecosystem, giving immediate access to newly launched meme tokens.
- **AMM-based trading** — Uses an automated market maker model to provide continuous liquidity without order books.
- **Solana speed** — Leverages Solana's high throughput and low transaction costs for fast, affordable swaps.
- **No account required** — Connect a wallet and trade instantly without registration or KYC.
- **Broad token support** — Access to the wide range of community and meme tokens launched through the pump.fun platform.
