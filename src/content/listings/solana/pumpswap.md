## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit swap.pump.fun and connect a Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom or Solflare.
2. **Select tokens** — Choose the token pair you want to swap, including tokens launched on the pump.fun launchpad.
3. **Enter an amount** — Input the amount you wish to swap and review the estimated output and price impact.
4. **Confirm the swap** — Approve the transaction in your wallet to execute the trade on-chain.

## Key Features

- **pump.fun Integration** — Directly connected to the pump.fun launchpad, giving immediate liquidity access to newly graduated tokens.
- **AMM-Based Swaps** — Uses an automated market maker model to facilitate permissionless token swaps without order books.
- **Solana Speed** — Leverages Solana's high-throughput, low-latency blockchain for fast and low-cost transactions.
- **Meme Token Focus** — Tailored for the meme token ecosystem that pump.fun has cultivated on Solana.
- **No Account Required** — Swap tokens directly from your wallet without creating an account or completing KYC.
