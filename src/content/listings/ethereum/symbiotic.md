## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit symbiotic.fi and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Choose an asset to restake** — Browse supported collateral assets and select which you want to deposit into a Symbiotic vault.
3. **Select a vault and operator** — Pick a vault aligned with your risk preferences; each vault delegates stake to one or more operators securing decentralized networks.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your restaked assets, accrued rewards, and slashing conditions through the Symbiotic dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Generalized Restaking** — Supports a broad range of ERC-20 collateral assets beyond just ETH, enabling flexible capital deployment.
- **Sovereign Network Support** — Decentralized networks can integrate Symbiotic to access shared security while retaining full control over their own logic and slashing rules.
- **Modular Vault Architecture** — Vaults are configurable and non-opinionated, allowing operators and curators to define custom risk and delegation parameters.
- **Permissionless Operator Registration** — Any operator can register and participate, fostering a competitive and open validator marketplace.
- **Slashing Customization** — Networks define their own slashing conditions, giving them sovereignty over how misbehavior is penalized.
- **Multi-Network Capital Efficiency** — Staked assets can simultaneously secure multiple networks, improving yield potential for depositors.
