## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [euler.finance](https://www.euler.finance) and connect a Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Choose a vault** — Browse available lending vaults or create a permissionless vault for any ERC-20 token.
3. **Deposit or borrow** — Supply assets to earn yield, or deposit collateral and borrow against it at market-determined rates.
4. **Monitor positions** — Track your health factor and rates directly in the Euler dashboard to manage risk.

## Key Features

- **Permissionless markets** — Any ERC-20 token can be listed as collateral or a lendable asset without requiring governance approval.
- **Modular vault architecture** — Euler V2 uses an EVC (Ethereum Vault Connector) system enabling composable, interoperable lending vaults.
- **Risk-adjusted rates** — Interest rates are algorithmically determined based on supply and demand, targeting competitive market rates.
- **Institutional-grade security** — The protocol has undergone multiple audits and implements layered risk controls to protect user funds.
- **Lend and build** — Developers can build custom lending products and integrations on top of the modular vault primitives.
- **Non-custodial** — Users retain control of their assets; all operations are governed by on-chain smart contracts.
