## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit euler.finance and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Choose or create a market** — Browse existing lending markets or deploy a new permissionless market for any ERC-20 asset.
3. **Supply or borrow** — Deposit assets to earn yield or provide collateral to borrow against at risk-adjusted rates.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and interest accrual through the Euler dashboard to manage risk.

## Key Features

- **Permissionless market creation** — Any ERC-20 token can be listed as collateral or a lendable asset without requiring governance approval.
- **Modular architecture** — The protocol is built with composable vault components, allowing flexible configuration of lending markets.
- **Risk-adjusted rates** — Interest rates adapt based on utilization and collateral risk profiles to balance lender and borrower incentives.
- **Institutional-grade security** — Euler V2 underwent extensive audits and formal verification following the V1 incident to prioritize safety.
- **Collateral flexibility** — Supports a wide range of assets as collateral, including long-tail tokens not typically found on major lending platforms.
- **Developer composability** — Builders can integrate Euler vaults into their own protocols and products using the modular vault interface.
