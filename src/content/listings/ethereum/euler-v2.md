## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit euler.finance and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse existing lending vaults or deploy your own permissionless market for any ERC-20 asset.
3. **Supply or borrow** — Deposit assets to earn yield or use your supplied collateral to borrow against your position.
4. **Monitor your health** — Keep an eye on your collateral ratio to avoid liquidation as market conditions change.

## Key Features

- **Modular Vault Architecture** — Each lending market is an isolated vault, allowing granular risk management and customization per asset.
- **Permissionless Market Creation** — Anyone can deploy a new lending market for any token without needing governance approval.
- **Any Asset as Collateral** — The protocol is designed to support a wide range of assets as collateral, expanding beyond blue-chip tokens.
- **Institutional-Grade Security** — Built with a focus on formal security practices, audits, and risk controls to support larger capital deployments.
- **EVC Integration** — Uses the Ethereum Vault Connector (EVC) to enable composable vault interactions across the ecosystem.
- **Flexible Interest Rate Models** — Market creators can configure custom interest rate curves suited to the specific asset and risk profile.
