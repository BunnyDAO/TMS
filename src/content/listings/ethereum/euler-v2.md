## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [euler.finance](https://www.euler.finance) and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Choose or create a vault** — Browse existing lending vaults or permissionlessly deploy a new vault for any ERC-20 asset.
3. **Deposit or borrow** — Supply assets to earn yield, or deposit collateral and borrow against it within a vault.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and collateral ratios through the dashboard to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Modular Vault Architecture** — Each lending market is an isolated vault, allowing granular risk management and customizable parameters per asset.
- **Permissionless Vault Creation** — Anyone can deploy a new lending vault for any ERC-20 token without needing governance approval.
- **Any Asset as Collateral** — The protocol is designed so that virtually any token can be listed and used as collateral in a lending market.
- **Composable by Design** — Vaults can be interconnected and composed, enabling developers to build complex lending products and strategies on top of the protocol.
- **Institutional-Grade Security** — Euler V2 is built with extensive audits and formal verification in mind, targeting institutional participants alongside retail users.
- **EVC Integration** — Built on the Ethereum Vault Connector (EVC) standard to enable cross-vault interactions and account-level position management.
