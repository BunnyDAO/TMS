## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [euler.finance](https://www.euler.finance) and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse existing lending markets or create a new permissionless market for any ERC-20 token.
3. **Supply or borrow** — Deposit assets to earn yield as a lender, or supply collateral and borrow against it.
4. **Monitor your position** — Keep track of your health factor and collateral ratios to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Permissionless Market Creation** — Anyone can deploy a new lending market for any ERC-20 asset without requiring governance approval.
- **Modular Architecture** — Markets can be configured independently with custom risk parameters, oracles, and interest rate models.
- **Any Asset as Collateral** — Unlike protocols with curated asset lists, Euler V2 allows long-tail and novel assets to be used as collateral in isolated markets.
- **Institutional-Grade Security** — The protocol has undergone extensive audits and incorporates robust security mechanisms to protect user funds.
- **Interconnected Vaults** — Vault-based design allows markets to be composed together, enabling more complex lending and borrowing strategies.
- **EVC Integration** — Built on the Ethereum Vault Connector, enabling cross-vault interactions and more expressive DeFi strategies.
