## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [euler.finance](https://www.euler.finance) and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Choose a vault** — Browse available lending markets or create a permissionless vault for any ERC-20 asset.
3. **Supply or borrow** — Deposit assets to earn yield as a lender, or provide collateral and borrow against it.
4. **Monitor positions** — Track your health factor, interest accrual, and collateral ratios directly in the dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Modular Vault Architecture** — Each market is an independent vault with configurable risk parameters, enabling fine-grained control over lending conditions.
- **Permissionless Market Creation** — Anyone can deploy a new lending market for any ERC-20 token without requiring governance approval.
- **Any Asset as Collateral** — Euler V2 extends collateral eligibility beyond blue-chip assets, allowing long-tail tokens to participate in lending markets.
- **Composable Building Blocks** — Developers can build custom lending products on top of Euler's vault primitives using the EVK (Euler Vault Kit).
- **Institutional-Grade Security** — The protocol incorporates multiple independent audits and a robust risk framework designed to protect both lenders and borrowers.
- **Flexible Interest Rate Models** — Vault creators can configure interest rate curves suited to the specific liquidity and risk profile of each asset.
