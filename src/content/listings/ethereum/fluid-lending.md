## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit fluid.io and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask or a smart wallet via Instadapp.
2. **Supply assets** — Deposit supported tokens into the lending pool to start earning yield from borrowers.
3. **Borrow against collateral** — Use your supplied assets as collateral to borrow other tokens at competitive rates.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and collateral ratio to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Unified liquidity** — Lending, DEX, and vault liquidity share the same pool, maximizing capital utilization across all protocol functions.
- **High capital efficiency** — Shared liquidity architecture reduces idle capital compared to siloed lending markets.
- **Integrated DEX** — Built-in trading layer allows liquidity to serve both lending and swapping simultaneously.
- **Vault system** — Structured vaults enable advanced borrowing and leverage strategies on top of the core lending layer.
- **Built by Instadapp** — Developed by the team behind Instadapp, a long-standing DeFi infrastructure provider with extensive smart contract experience.
- **Earn yield on deposits** — Suppliers earn interest from borrowers, with rates driven by pool utilization.
