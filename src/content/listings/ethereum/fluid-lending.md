## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit fluid.io and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or a wallet via WalletConnect.
2. **Choose an asset** — Browse the available lending markets and select an asset you wish to supply or borrow against.
3. **Supply collateral** — Deposit supported tokens to earn yield or to use as collateral for borrowing.
4. **Borrow or earn** — Borrow against your collateral or simply leave assets supplied to accumulate lending interest.

## Key Features

- **Unified Liquidity Layer** — Fluid routes liquidity across lending and other DeFi use cases from a single shared pool, improving capital efficiency.
- **Lending Markets** — Supply crypto assets to earn variable interest rates determined by supply and demand dynamics.
- **Borrowing** — Take out loans against your collateral with configurable loan-to-value ratios and liquidation thresholds.
- **Instadapp Integration** — Built by the team behind Instadapp, leveraging years of DeFi infrastructure experience.
- **Evolving Protocol** — Fluid is designed to expand its financial primitives over time, adding new market types and features progressively.
- **Non-Custodial** — Users retain control of their assets through smart contracts with no centralized intermediary.
