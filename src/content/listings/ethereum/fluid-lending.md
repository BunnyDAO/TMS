## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [fluid.io](https://fluid.io/lending/1) and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Choose an asset to supply** — Browse available markets and select a token to deposit. Supplied assets begin earning yield immediately.
3. **Borrow against collateral** — Once you have supplied collateral, you can borrow supported assets up to your available borrowing limit.
4. **Monitor your position** — Keep an eye on your health factor and collateralization ratio to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Capital Efficiency** — Advanced mechanisms allow more borrowing power per unit of collateral compared to traditional DeFi lending designs.
- **Yield Generation** — Suppliers earn competitive interest rates from borrower demand across supported asset markets.
- **Built by Instadapp** — Developed by the team behind one of DeFi's most established infrastructure providers, bringing deep protocol expertise.
- **Ever-Evolving Design** — The protocol is actively developed with iterative improvements to liquidity and risk management.
- **Non-Custodial** — Users retain control of their assets through smart contracts with no central intermediary.
- **Significant TVL** — With ~$879M in total value locked, Fluid Lending has established meaningful liquidity depth for users.
