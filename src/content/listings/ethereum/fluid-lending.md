## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit fluid.io and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or a smart wallet powered by Instadapp.
2. **Supply assets** — Choose a supported asset to deposit into the Fluid liquidity layer and begin earning lending yield.
3. **Borrow against collateral** — Use your supplied assets as collateral to borrow other tokens, subject to the protocol's loan-to-value parameters.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and interest rates directly in the Fluid dashboard to manage liquidation risk.

## Key Features

- **Unified Liquidity Layer** — All markets share a single liquidity pool, improving capital efficiency and reducing idle funds across isolated markets.
- **Advanced Lending Mechanics** — Supports more sophisticated borrowing structures compared to first-generation money markets, enabling better rates and risk management.
- **Instadapp Integration** — Built by the team behind Instadapp, enabling seamless composability with DeFi automation and smart account infrastructure.
- **Improved Capital Efficiency** — Users can put collateral to work more effectively, with less liquidity fragmentation than traditional isolated lending pools.
- **Non-Custodial** — Funds remain in smart contracts; users retain control of their assets throughout the lending and borrowing process.
