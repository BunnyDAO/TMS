## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [app.morpho.org](https://app.morpho.org) and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Browse markets** — Explore available isolated lending markets, each defined by a specific collateral/loan asset pair and oracle configuration.
3. **Supply or borrow** — Deposit assets to earn yield as a lender, or supply collateral and borrow against it in a chosen market.
4. **Monitor positions** — Track your loan-to-value ratio and accrued interest directly in the interface to manage liquidation risk.

## Key Features

- **Permissionless market creation** — Anyone can create a new lending market by specifying collateral asset, loan asset, oracle, and liquidation parameters without governance approval.
- **Isolated risk** — Each market is independent, meaning bad debt or asset failures in one market do not affect others.
- **Minimal and trustless** — The core protocol is immutable and contains no admin keys, reducing smart contract and governance risk.
- **Customizable parameters** — Market creators can set loan-to-value ratios, liquidation incentives, and oracle sources to suit specific asset profiles.
- **Composable primitive** — Designed for other protocols and vault curators to build on top of, enabling products like MetaMorpho vaults for passive lending.
- **Open-source contracts** — All smart contracts are publicly available and audited, allowing independent verification of the protocol logic.
