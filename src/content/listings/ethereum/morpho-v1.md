## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.morpho.org and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse existing lending markets or create a permissionless market with your preferred collateral and loan asset pair.
3. **Supply or borrow** — Deposit assets to earn yield as a lender, or provide collateral and borrow against it at market-determined rates.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and interest accrual directly in the dashboard to manage liquidation risk.

## Key Features

- **Permissionless Market Creation** — Anyone can deploy a new lending market with custom collateral, loan tokens, liquidation LTV, and oracle settings without requiring governance approval.
- **Isolated Markets** — Each market is independent, containing risk to specific asset pairs rather than pooling all assets together.
- **Trustless Architecture** — Smart contracts are immutable and non-upgradeable, removing reliance on admin keys or protocol governance for core operations.
- **Efficient Capital Utilization** — Interest rates adjust to supply and demand within each market, helping lenders maximize yield and borrowers find competitive rates.
- **MetaMorpho Vaults** — Curated vault layer built on top of Morpho that lets passive lenders deposit into managed strategies across multiple markets.
- **Flexible Oracle Support** — Markets can integrate any price oracle, giving creators flexibility to support a wide range of assets.
