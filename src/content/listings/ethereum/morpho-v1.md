## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.morpho.org and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse existing lending markets filtered by collateral type, loan asset, or yield to find one that fits your needs.
3. **Supply or borrow** — Deposit collateral to borrow assets, or supply assets directly into a market to earn interest from borrowers.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and collateral ratio to avoid liquidation, adjusting your position as market conditions change.

## Key Features

- **Permissionless market creation** — Anyone can deploy a new lending market by specifying a collateral asset, loan asset, LTV parameters, and a price oracle without governance approval.
- **Isolated risk** — Each market is independent, so risk from one collateral asset does not spill over into unrelated markets.
- **Efficient lending primitive** — Morpho is designed as a low-level building block that curators and protocols can build upon to create higher-level lending products.
- **No protocol-managed risk** — Risk parameters are set at market creation and are immutable, reducing reliance on ongoing governance decisions.
- **Open-source contracts** — All smart contracts are publicly available and audited, enabling transparency and composability with other DeFi protocols.
- **High TVL and liquidity** — Morpho consistently ranks among the top DeFi lending protocols by total value locked on Ethereum.
