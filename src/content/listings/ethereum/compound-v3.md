## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit the Compound V3 dashboard and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Supply collateral** — Deposit supported collateral assets (e.g., ETH, WBTC, LINK) into the protocol to back your position.
3. **Borrow or earn** — Either borrow the base asset (e.g., USDC) against your collateral, or supply the base asset directly to earn interest.
4. **Monitor your position** — Keep an eye on your collateral ratio via the dashboard and repay or add collateral as needed to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Single base asset model** — Each Compound V3 market is organized around one borrowable base asset, simplifying risk isolation compared to pooled multi-asset models.
- **Autonomous interest rates** — Interest rates adjust algorithmically based on real-time supply and demand, requiring no manual intervention.
- **Multi-chain deployment** — Compound V3 is deployed across Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, and other networks, broadening access.
- **Community governance** — COMP token holders can propose and vote on protocol changes, collateral listings, and risk parameters.
- **Improved risk management** — The redesigned architecture limits contagion risk by isolating collateral assets from the borrowable base asset.
- **Open-source contracts** — The Comet smart contracts are fully open-source and audited, enabling transparency and third-party integrations.
