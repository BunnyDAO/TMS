## Getting Started

1. **Connect a wallet** — Visit the Compound dashboard and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Choose a market** — Select a supported chain and market (e.g., USDC on Ethereum) to supply or borrow against.
3. **Supply or borrow** — Deposit a base asset to earn interest, or supply collateral and borrow the base asset against it.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your collateral ratio and accrued interest directly from the dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Algorithmic interest rates** — Rates are set automatically by on-chain supply and demand, requiring no manual intervention.
- **Single base asset model** — Each market is built around one borrowable base asset, simplifying risk management compared to earlier versions.
- **Multi-chain deployment** — Protocol is deployed across Ethereum and additional EVM-compatible networks.
- **Community governance** — COMP token holders can propose and vote on protocol upgrades and parameter changes.
- **Collateral management** — Users can supply multiple accepted collateral assets to back borrowing positions.
- **Dashboard interface** — A full-featured web UI provides real-time position tracking, market data, and governance access.
