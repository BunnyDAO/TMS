## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit ethena.fi and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Mint USDe** — Deposit supported crypto collateral (such as ETH or staked ETH) to mint USDe at a 1:1 peg to the US dollar.
3. **Stake USDe** — Stake your USDe to receive sUSDe and begin earning yield generated from the delta-neutral derivatives strategy.
4. **Redeem or trade** — Unstake and redeem USDe back to your collateral at any time, or trade USDe across supported DeFi protocols.

## Key Features

- **Synthetic Dollar (USDe)** — A crypto-native stablecoin pegged to USD, backed by delta-neutral derivatives positions rather than fiat reserves or traditional banking.
- **Delta-Neutral Backing** — USDe maintains its peg through offsetting long spot and short perpetual futures positions, reducing directional price risk.
- **Staking Yield (sUSDe)** — Users can stake USDe to earn protocol-generated yield from the underlying derivatives funding rates and staking rewards.
- **Internet Bond** — A globally accessible, dollar-denominated yield instrument designed as a crypto-native alternative to traditional savings products.
- **No Banking Dependency** — The protocol is designed to operate entirely on-chain without reliance on traditional financial infrastructure or fiat custodians.
- **DeFi Composability** — USDe and sUSDe are ERC-20 tokens usable across the broader Ethereum DeFi ecosystem including lending, liquidity, and trading protocols.
