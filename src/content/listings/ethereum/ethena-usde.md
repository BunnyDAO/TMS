## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.ethena.fi and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Mint USDe** — Deposit supported collateral assets (such as stETH or USDC) to mint USDe at a 1:1 dollar peg.
3. **Stake for yield** — Stake your USDe to receive sUSDe, which automatically accrues protocol-generated yield over time.
4. **Monitor and redeem** — Track your sUSDe balance and rewards in the dashboard, and redeem back to USDe or underlying collateral at any time.

## Key Features

- **USDe Synthetic Dollar** — A crypto-native stable asset pegged to $1, backed by delta-neutral positions rather than fiat reserves or overcollateralized loans.
- **Delta-Neutral Backing** — Combines staked ETH as collateral with short perpetual futures positions to hedge price exposure and maintain the peg.
- **sUSDe Yield Instrument** — Staking USDe yields sUSDe, an interest-bearing token that captures funding rate income and staking rewards as the 'Internet Bond'.
- **No Traditional Banking Dependency** — Designed to function without reliance on traditional financial infrastructure, reducing counterparty and censorship risk.
- **Ethereum-Native** — Fully on-chain protocol deployable and composable with other DeFi protocols across the Ethereum ecosystem.
- **Transparent Collateral** — Collateral positions and backing are publicly verifiable on-chain, providing auditability of the peg mechanism.
