## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.ethena.fi and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Mint USDe** — Deposit accepted collateral assets (such as stETH or other supported tokens) to mint USDe at a 1:1 dollar peg.
3. **Stake USDe for yield** — Stake your USDe to receive sUSDe, which accrues yield generated from the delta-neutral derivatives strategy backing the protocol.
4. **Redeem or trade** — Unstake and redeem USDe back to collateral at any time, or trade USDe across supported DeFi platforms.

## Key Features

- **Delta-Neutral Backing** — USDe maintains its peg through offsetting derivatives positions rather than relying on fiat reserves or overcollateralized loans.
- **Native Yield** — Staking USDe generates protocol yield derived from funding rates and staking rewards on the underlying collateral.
- **Internet Bond** — A globally accessible, dollar-denominated savings instrument that provides on-chain yield without dependence on traditional banking rails.
- **Crypto-Native Stability** — The protocol is designed to remain independent of traditional financial infrastructure, reducing counterparty and censorship risk.
- **Scalable Supply** — USDe supply can expand and contract efficiently as the protocol mints and redeems based on market demand and collateral availability.
