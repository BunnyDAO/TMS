## Getting Started

1. **Connect a wallet** — Visit app.ethena.fi and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Mint USDe** — Deposit supported collateral assets (such as stETH or USDT) to mint USDe, Ethena's synthetic dollar.
3. **Stake for yield** — Stake your USDe to receive sUSDe, an interest-bearing token that accumulates protocol-generated rewards over time.
4. **Monitor positions** — Track your holdings, accrued yield, and protocol stats directly from the Ethena dashboard.

## Key Features

- **USDe Synthetic Dollar** — A crypto-native stablecoin pegged to the US dollar, backed by staked ETH and short perpetual positions rather than fiat reserves.
- **Delta-Neutral Design** — The protocol hedges collateral price exposure using short perpetual futures, maintaining a stable peg without reliance on traditional banks.
- **sUSDe Yield Token** — Staking USDe produces sUSDe, which automatically accrues yield derived from staking rewards and funding rate income.
- **Internet Bond** — Ethena positions sUSDe as a globally accessible, dollar-denominated savings instrument outside the traditional financial system.
- **Non-Custodial** — Users interact directly with on-chain smart contracts; collateral is held with regulated off-exchange custodians rather than centralized platforms.
- **Transparent Backing** — Collateral positions and protocol reserves are publicly verifiable on-chain and through Ethena's real-time dashboards.
