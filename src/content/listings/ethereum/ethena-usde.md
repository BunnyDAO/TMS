## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit ethena.fi and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Mint USDe** — Deposit supported collateral assets (such as stETH or USDT) to mint USDe at a 1:1 dollar peg.
3. **Stake for yield** — Convert USDe to sUSDe to earn yield generated from the protocol's delta-neutral hedging strategy and staking rewards.
4. **Redeem or trade** — Redeem USDe back for underlying collateral directly through the app, or trade USDe on supported DEXs and CEXs.

## Key Features

- **Delta-Neutral Backing** — USDe maintains its peg by pairing staked ETH collateral with short ETH perpetual futures positions, eliminating directional price exposure.
- **sUSDe Internet Bond** — Staking USDe into sUSDe generates yield from ETH staking rewards and perpetual funding rates, creating a crypto-native savings instrument.
- **No Traditional Banking Dependency** — The protocol operates entirely on-chain without reliance on traditional banking infrastructure or fiat reserves.
- **Large Scale Liquidity** — With $4.3B+ TVL, USDe benefits from deep liquidity across major DeFi protocols and centralized exchanges.
- **Transparent Collateral** — All backing positions and collateral are verifiable on-chain, providing real-time proof of reserves.
- **DeFi Integrations** — USDe and sUSDe are widely integrated across lending protocols, DEXs, and yield platforms throughout the Ethereum ecosystem.
