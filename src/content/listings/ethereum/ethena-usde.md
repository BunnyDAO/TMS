## Getting Started

1. **Connect a wallet** — Visit ethena.fi and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Mint USDe** — Deposit accepted collateral assets (such as ETH, stETH, or USDT) to mint USDe at a 1:1 dollar peg.
3. **Stake for yield** — Stake your USDe to receive sUSDe (staked USDe) and begin earning yield derived from derivatives funding rates.
4. **Redeem anytime** — Unstake sUSDe and redeem USDe back to underlying collateral at any time through the protocol interface.

## Key Features

- **Delta-Neutral Collateral** — USDe is backed by spot crypto assets hedged with short perpetual futures positions, maintaining price stability without relying on fiat reserves.
- **Yield-Bearing Stablecoin** — Staking USDe mints sUSDe, which accrues yield from funding rate income generated by the hedging strategy.
- **Internet Bond** — Ethena frames sUSDe as a globally accessible, dollar-denominated yield instrument independent of traditional banking rails.
- **Full Collateralization** — All USDe in circulation is backed 1:1 by collateral held transparently on-chain and with regulated custodians.
- **No Banking Dependency** — The protocol operates without reliance on traditional bank accounts or fiat infrastructure, reducing counterparty risk from the banking system.
