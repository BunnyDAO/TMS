## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit ethena.fi and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Mint USDe** — Deposit accepted collateral (such as ETH, stETH, or USDT) to mint USDe at a 1:1 dollar peg.
3. **Stake for yield** — Stake your USDe to receive sUSDe, which accrues yield derived from derivatives funding rates.
4. **Redeem anytime** — Unstake sUSDe and redeem USDe back to underlying collateral through the protocol interface.

## Key Features

- **Synthetic Dollar (USDe)** — A crypto-native stablecoin pegged to the US dollar, not reliant on traditional banking or fiat reserves.
- **Delta-Neutral Backing** — USDe is collateralized by crypto assets hedged with short perpetual futures positions, neutralizing price exposure.
- **Yield Generation (sUSDe)** — Staked USDe earns yield from positive funding rates in perpetuals markets, functioning as an 'Internet Bond'.
- **No Banking Dependency** — The protocol operates entirely on-chain without reliance on traditional financial infrastructure.
- **Large TVL** — With over $4.3B in total value locked, Ethena is one of the largest stablecoin protocols in DeFi.
- **Internet Bond** — sUSDe serves as a globally accessible, dollar-denominated yield instrument for anyone with an Ethereum wallet.
