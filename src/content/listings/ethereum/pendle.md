## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [pendle.finance](https://pendle.finance) and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse available yield markets for assets like stETH, aUSDC, or other supported yield-bearing tokens.
3. **Select your strategy** — Buy PT (Principal Tokens) to lock in a fixed rate, or buy YT (Yield Tokens) to speculate on variable yield performance.
4. **Manage your position** — Monitor your holdings from the portfolio dashboard and exit or roll positions as maturity dates approach.

## Key Features

- **Yield Tokenization** — Splits yield-bearing assets into Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), enabling separate trading of each component.
- **Fixed Rate Locking** — Purchase PT at a discount to lock in a guaranteed fixed return by the token's maturity date, regardless of market fluctuations.
- **Yield Speculation** — Buy YT to gain leveraged exposure to the yield generated by an underlying asset over a given period.
- **Specialized AMM** — A custom automated market maker designed specifically for yield trading, accounting for time decay as tokens approach maturity.
- **Multi-chain Support** — Deployed on Ethereum, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, and other EVM-compatible networks.
- **Liquidity Provision** — LPs can deposit into Pendle pools to earn swap fees and PENDLE token incentives.
