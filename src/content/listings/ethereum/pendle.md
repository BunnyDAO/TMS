## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit pendle.finance and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse available yield markets for assets like stETH, aUSDC, or other supported yield-bearing tokens.
3. **Mint or buy PT/YT tokens** — Wrap a yield-bearing token to split it into a Principal Token (PT) and a Yield Token (YT), or purchase them directly on the AMM.
4. **Manage your position** — Hold PT to lock in a fixed return at maturity, trade YT to gain leveraged yield exposure, or provide liquidity to earn fees.

## Key Features

- **Yield Tokenization** — Splits any supported yield-bearing token into a fixed Principal Token (PT) and a variable Yield Token (YT), enabling independent trading of each component.
- **Specialized AMM** — A custom automated market maker designed for yield assets, minimizing slippage and impermanent loss as tokens approach maturity.
- **Fixed-Rate Yield** — Users can buy PT at a discount and hold to maturity to lock in a predictable fixed return without active management.
- **Yield Speculation** — YT holders receive all yield generated by the underlying asset, offering amplified exposure to rising yield environments.
- **Liquidity Provision** — LPs can deposit assets into Pendle pools to earn swap fees and PENDLE token incentives.
- **Multi-Chain Support** — Deployed on Ethereum, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, and other EVM networks, broadening access to yield markets across ecosystems.
