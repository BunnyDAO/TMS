## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit pendle.finance and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse available yield markets for assets like stETH, aUSDC, or other yield-bearing tokens.
3. **Split or trade** — Deposit a yield-bearing asset to receive Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), or trade them directly on the AMM.
4. **Manage your position** — Hold PT to redeem the principal at maturity for a fixed return, or hold YT to capture all future yield generated by the underlying asset.

## Key Features

- **Yield Tokenization** — Splits yield-bearing assets into PT (principal) and YT (yield) components, separating ownership of yield from the underlying asset.
- **Specialized AMM** — A custom automated market maker designed specifically for yield trading, accounting for yield token time-decay in its pricing model.
- **Fixed-Rate Exposure** — Users can lock in a fixed yield by purchasing PT at a discount, guaranteeing a known return at maturity.
- **Yield Speculation** — YT holders receive all yield generated by the underlying asset, enabling leveraged exposure to yield rate movements.
- **Multi-Chain Support** — Available on Ethereum, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, and other networks, with liquidity pools across major yield-bearing assets.
- **Liquidity Provision** — LPs can supply liquidity to Pendle pools and earn swap fees plus PENDLE token incentives.
