## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit pendle.finance and connect a Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby to get started.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse available yield markets, which include assets like stETH, sUSDe, and other yield-bearing tokens across Ethereum and supported L2s.
3. **Split or trade yield** — Deposit a yield-bearing asset to receive Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), or trade them directly on the AMM to lock in fixed rates or take leveraged yield positions.
4. **Provide liquidity** — Add liquidity to Pendle pools to earn swap fees and incentives on top of the underlying yield.

## Key Features

- **Principal Tokens (PT)** — Represent the principal of a yield-bearing asset redeemable at maturity, allowing users to effectively lock in a fixed yield.
- **Yield Tokens (YT)** — Represent the right to receive all future yield from an asset until expiry, enabling leveraged exposure to yield movements.
- **Specialized AMM** — A custom AMM designed for time-decaying yield assets, providing efficient pricing as tokens approach maturity.
- **Multi-chain support** — Available on Ethereum mainnet, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Optimism, and other networks.
- **vePENDLE governance** — PENDLE token holders can lock tokens for vePENDLE to vote on gauge weights and earn a share of protocol revenue.
- **Broad asset coverage** — Supports a wide range of yield sources including liquid staking tokens, RWA yield, and money market positions.
