## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit pendle.finance and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse available yield markets and select a yield-bearing asset (e.g., stETH, aUSDC) whose yield you want to trade.
3. **Split or trade** — Either split your asset into Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), or trade them directly on the Pendle AMM.
4. **Manage your position** — Monitor your fixed-yield or yield-speculating position and redeem at maturity or exit early via the AMM.

## Key Features

- **Yield Tokenization** — Splits yield-bearing assets into separate Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), enabling independent trading of each component.
- **Fixed Yield** — Users can buy PT at a discount to lock in a guaranteed return at maturity, similar to a zero-coupon bond.
- **Yield Speculation** — YT holders receive all yield generated by the underlying asset, allowing leveraged exposure to future yield rates.
- **Custom AMM** — Pendle's AMM is designed specifically for yield trading, accounting for the time-decaying nature of yield tokens to reduce impermanent loss.
- **Multi-chain Support** — Available on Ethereum, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, and other networks, covering a broad range of yield-bearing assets.
- **vePENDLE Governance** — PENDLE token holders can lock tokens to earn protocol fees, boost LP rewards, and vote on gauge allocations.
