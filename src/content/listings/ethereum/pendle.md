## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit pendle.finance and connect a compatible Ethereum or EVM wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Select a yield-bearing asset** — Choose from supported assets like stETH, aUSDC, or other yield-bearing tokens to interact with.
3. **Split or trade yield** — Wrap your asset into Pendle to receive Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), or swap directly on the Pendle AMM.
4. **Manage your position** — Hold PT for fixed-rate exposure at maturity, trade YT to speculate on yield, or provide liquidity to earn swap fees.

## Key Features

- **Yield Tokenization** — Splits yield-bearing assets into separate Principal Tokens and Yield Tokens, enabling independent trading of each component.
- **Fixed-Rate Exposure** — Users can purchase Principal Tokens at a discount to lock in a guaranteed return at maturity, similar to zero-coupon bonds.
- **Yield Speculation** — Yield Tokens give leveraged exposure to the underlying asset's yield rate, allowing traders to go long or short on future APY.
- **Specialized AMM** — Pendle's custom AMM is designed specifically for yield token trading, accounting for time decay as tokens approach maturity.
- **Liquidity Provision** — LPs can deposit assets into Pendle pools to earn swap fees and PENDLE token incentives.
- **Multi-Chain Support** — Available on Ethereum, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, and other EVM networks, with integrations across major yield sources.
