## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit pendle.finance and connect a Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby on a supported chain (Ethereum, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, etc.).
2. **Choose a market** — Browse available yield markets and select an asset whose future yield you want to trade or lock in.
3. **Split or trade yield** — Deposit a yield-bearing asset to mint Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), or buy/sell these directly on the Pendle AMM.
4. **Manage your position** — Hold PT to redeem the principal at maturity with a fixed return, trade YT to speculate on variable yield, or provide liquidity to earn fees.

## Key Features

- **Yield Tokenization** — Splits yield-bearing assets into Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), enabling independent trading of each component.
- **Fixed-Rate Yields** — Users can buy PT at a discount to lock in a predictable fixed return by maturity, removing exposure to variable rate fluctuations.
- **Yield Speculation** — YT holders gain leveraged exposure to the underlying yield rate, allowing directional bets on rising or falling yields.
- **Specialized AMM** — Pendle's custom AMM is designed specifically for time-decaying yield assets, providing efficient pricing as tokens approach maturity.
- **Multi-Chain Support** — Deployed across Ethereum mainnet, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Optimism, and other networks, with a broad range of supported yield sources.
- **Liquidity Provision** — LPs can deposit assets into Pendle pools to earn trading fees and PENDLE token incentives.
