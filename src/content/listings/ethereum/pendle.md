## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [pendle.finance](https://pendle.finance/) and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse available yield markets for assets like stETH, aUSDC, or other yield-bearing tokens.
3. **Select your strategy** — Buy Principal Tokens (PT) to lock in a fixed yield at maturity, or buy Yield Tokens (YT) to gain leveraged exposure to variable yield rates.
4. **Provide liquidity (optional)** — Deposit into Pendle's AMM liquidity pools to earn trading fees and PENDLE token incentives.

## Key Features

- **Yield Tokenization** — Splits yield-bearing assets into separate Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT) for independent trading.
- **Fixed-Rate Yields** — Users can purchase PT at a discount to lock in a guaranteed return by the token's maturity date.
- **Yield Speculation** — YT holders gain leveraged exposure to a yield-bearing asset's variable APY without holding the full principal.
- **Specialized AMM** — A custom AMM designed to handle the time-decaying value of yield tokens efficiently and with low slippage.
- **Multi-Chain Support** — Deployed on Ethereum mainnet, Arbitrum, BSC, and other networks for broad asset coverage.
- **PENDLE Incentives** — Liquidity providers and vePENDLE holders can earn protocol fees and token rewards.
