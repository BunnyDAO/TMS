## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit pendle.finance and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse available yield markets and select a yield-bearing asset you want to interact with (e.g., stETH, aUSDC).
3. **Select your strategy** — Buy Principal Tokens (PT) to lock in a fixed yield, or buy Yield Tokens (YT) to speculate on variable yield going higher.
4. **Provide liquidity (optional)** — Deposit into Pendle's AMM liquidity pools to earn trading fees and PENDLE incentives.

## Key Features

- **Yield Tokenization** — Yield-bearing assets are split into Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), enabling independent trading of each component.
- **Fixed-Rate Yields** — Purchase PT at a discount to lock in a guaranteed return at maturity, regardless of market fluctuations.
- **Yield Speculation** — Hold YT to capture all future yield generated by the underlying asset, amplifying exposure to variable rates.
- **Specialized AMM** — Pendle's custom AMM is designed specifically for yield tokens, accounting for time decay as assets approach maturity.
- **vePENDLE Governance** — Lock PENDLE tokens to receive vePENDLE, granting governance rights, boosted rewards, and a share of protocol fees.
- **Multi-chain Support** — Pendle is deployed across Ethereum, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, and other EVM networks, broadening access to yield markets.
