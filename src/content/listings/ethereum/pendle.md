## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit pendle.finance and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse available yield markets for assets like stETH, rETH, or other yield-bearing tokens across supported chains.
3. **Split or trade yield** — Deposit a yield-bearing asset to receive Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), or trade them directly on the AMM to fix or speculate on yield.
4. **Provide liquidity** — Supply liquidity to Pendle's AMM pools to earn trading fees and PENDLE token incentives.

## Key Features

- **Yield Tokenization** — Splits yield-bearing assets into Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), allowing independent trading of each component.
- **Fixed-Rate Exposure** — Buy PT at a discount to lock in a fixed return by maturity, removing exposure to variable yield fluctuations.
- **Yield Speculation** — Purchase YT to gain leveraged exposure to the yield generated by an underlying asset without holding the principal.
- **Custom AMM** — Pendle's AMM is designed to handle the time-decay nature of yield tokens, providing efficient pricing as assets approach maturity.
- **Multi-Chain Support** — Available on Ethereum mainnet, Arbitrum, BSC, and other networks, broadening access to yield markets.
- **PENDLE Governance Token** — vePENDLE holders can vote on gauge weights, boosting liquidity rewards and participating in protocol governance.
