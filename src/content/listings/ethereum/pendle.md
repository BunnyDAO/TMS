## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit pendle.finance and connect a Web3 wallet such as MetaMask to a supported network like Ethereum or Arbitrum.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse available yield markets and select a yield-bearing asset (e.g., stETH, aUSDC) to interact with.
3. **Split or trade** — Deposit your yield-bearing asset to receive Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), or trade them directly on the Pendle AMM.
4. **Manage your position** — Hold PT to receive the underlying asset at maturity with fixed yield, or hold YT to capture all future yield generated by the asset.

## Key Features

- **Yield Tokenization** — Splits yield-bearing assets into PT (principal) and YT (yield) components, enabling independent management of each.
- **Fixed Yield** — Users can lock in a fixed rate of return by purchasing PT at a discount and redeeming it at face value upon maturity.
- **Yield Trading AMM** — A purpose-built AMM optimized for time-decaying yield assets, providing efficient pricing and liquidity for PT and YT markets.
- **Leverage Yield Exposure** — YT holders receive all the yield generated by the underlying principal, enabling amplified yield strategies with less capital.
- **Multi-chain Support** — Pendle markets are available across Ethereum, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, and other networks.
- **Liquidity Provision** — Users can provide liquidity to Pendle pools to earn trading fees and PENDLE token incentives.
