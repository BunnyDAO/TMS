## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit pendle.finance and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse available yield markets for assets like stETH, eETH, or other yield-bearing tokens.
3. **Split or trade yield** — Deposit a yield-bearing asset to receive Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT), or trade directly on the AMM.
4. **Manage your position** — Monitor your fixed or variable yield exposure and redeem tokens at maturity.

## Key Features

- **Yield Tokenization** — Splits yield-bearing assets into Principal Tokens (PT) and Yield Tokens (YT) for independent trading
- **Fixed Yield** — Buy PT at a discount to lock in a fixed return by maturity, regardless of variable rate movements
- **Yield Speculation** — Hold YT to gain leveraged exposure to the yield or points generated by an underlying asset
- **Purpose-Built AMM** — A custom AMM designed specifically for time-decaying yield assets, minimizing impermanent loss near maturity
- **Points Farming** — Accumulate protocol points from LSTs and restaking assets through YT positions
- **Multi-Chain Support** — Available on Ethereum mainnet, Arbitrum, BSC, Optimism, and other networks
