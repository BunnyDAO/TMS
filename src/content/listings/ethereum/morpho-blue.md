## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.morpho.org and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby.
2. **Browse markets** — Explore existing lending markets filtered by asset pair, oracle, and risk parameters to find one that fits your needs.
3. **Supply or borrow** — Deposit collateral to borrow against it, or supply assets directly to earn yield from borrowers in that market.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and interest accrual from the dashboard and manage your position as market conditions change.

## Key Features

- **Permissionless market creation** — Anyone can deploy a new lending market by specifying a loan asset, collateral asset, oracle, and LTV parameters without requiring protocol governance approval.
- **Isolated risk** — Each market is fully isolated, meaning exposure is limited to the specific assets and parameters of that market rather than pooled across the protocol.
- **Trustless design** — Smart contracts are minimal and immutable, reducing governance attack surface and reliance on upgradeable proxies.
- **Oracle flexibility** — Market creators choose their own price oracle, enabling support for a wide range of assets beyond established blue-chip tokens.
- **Efficient liquidations** — A streamlined liquidation mechanism keeps the protocol solvent while minimizing unnecessary losses for borrowers.
- **Composable base layer** — Morpho Blue is designed to be built upon, enabling curated vaults and aggregators like MetaMorpho to abstract complexity for end users.
