## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.morpho.org and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse existing lending markets filtered by collateral asset, loan asset, or yield rate.
3. **Supply or borrow** — Deposit collateral to borrow assets, or supply assets to a market to earn interest from borrowers.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and manage collateral ratios to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Permissionless market creation** — Anyone can deploy a new lending market with custom collateral, loan asset, oracle, and liquidation loan-to-value settings without governance approval.
- **Isolated markets** — Each market is independent, so risk from one asset pair does not affect others on the protocol.
- **Trustless design** — Core protocol parameters are immutable after deployment, eliminating governance risk on existing markets.
- **Capital efficiency** — The minimal protocol design reduces overhead and allows for competitive interest rates for both lenders and borrowers.
- **Open source** — The smart contract code is publicly available and audited, enabling developers to build products and vaults on top of Morpho Blue.
- **MetaMorpho vaults** — Curated risk managers can build managed vault products on top of Morpho Blue, abstracting market selection for passive lenders.
