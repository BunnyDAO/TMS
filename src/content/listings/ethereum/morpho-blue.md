## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.morpho.org and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Choose a market** — Browse existing lending markets filtered by asset pair, collateral type, or yield rate.
3. **Supply or borrow** — Deposit assets to earn yield, or provide collateral to take out a loan against your holdings.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and interest accrual directly in the dashboard to manage liquidation risk.

## Key Features

- **Permissionless market creation** — Anyone can deploy a new lending market by specifying a loan asset, collateral asset, oracle, and liquidation loan-to-value ratio.
- **Isolated risk** — Each market operates independently, so exposure from one asset pair does not affect others on the protocol.
- **Immutable core contracts** — The Morpho Blue core is non-upgradeable, removing governance risk from the foundational lending logic.
- **Capital efficiency** — Lenders and borrowers interact directly with minimal intermediary overhead, resulting in tighter spreads than pooled models.
- **Oracle flexibility** — Markets can integrate any price oracle, allowing support for a wide range of assets including long-tail tokens.
- **Composable primitive** — Designed to be built upon by other DeFi protocols, vaults, and aggregators seeking a trustless lending layer.
