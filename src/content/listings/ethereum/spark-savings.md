## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit data.spark.fi/savings and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Acquire USDS** — Obtain USDS stablecoins through a supported exchange or the Sky protocol.
3. **Deposit into savings** — Deposit your USDS into the Spark Savings contract to begin accruing the Sky Savings Rate.
4. **Monitor your yield** — Track your earnings and overall savings activity in real time via the Spark Data Hub dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Sky Savings Rate** — Earn a transparent, predictable APY by depositing USDS into the savings contract.
- **Large TVL** — Over $2.9B in total value locked, reflecting significant protocol adoption and liquidity depth.
- **Real-time analytics** — The Spark Data Hub provides live insights into savings balances, rates, and lending activity.
- **Stablecoin-native** — Designed specifically for USDS, minimizing exposure to volatile asset price risk while earning yield.
- **Ethereum-based** — Built on Ethereum, benefiting from the network's security and broad wallet compatibility.
- **Non-custodial** — Users retain control of their assets through smart contract interactions, with no centralized custodian.
