## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit the Spark Savings dashboard and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Deposit stablecoins** — Convert supported stablecoins to USDS and deposit them into the USDS Savings contract.
3. **Earn the Sky Savings Rate** — Your deposited USDS begins accruing yield automatically at the current Sky Savings Rate APY.
4. **Monitor performance** — Use the Spark Data Hub dashboard to track real-time savings activity and your accrued returns.

## Key Features

- **Sky Savings Rate** — Earn a predictable, protocol-set APY on deposited USDS managed by Sky governance.
- **Stablecoin deposits** — Deposit common stablecoins that are converted into USDS upon entry, simplifying the onboarding process.
- **Real-time analytics** — The Spark Data Hub provides live insights into savings rates, total deposits, and protocol activity.
- **Non-custodial** — Funds remain in a decentralized smart contract, with users retaining control of their assets.
- **Passive yield** — No active management required; yield accrues automatically once funds are deposited.
