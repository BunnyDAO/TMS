## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.spark.fi and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Deposit stablecoins** — Select a supported stablecoin (such as DAI or USDC) and enter the amount you want to deposit.
3. **Receive USDS** — Your deposit is converted into USDS, which automatically accrues the Sky Savings Rate over time.
4. **Withdraw anytime** — There are no lockup periods; redeem your USDS back to stablecoins whenever you choose.

## Key Features

- **Sky Savings Rate** — Earn a transparent, protocol-set APY on stablecoin deposits without active management.
- **USDS Integration** — Deposits are held as USDS, the native stablecoin of the Sky ecosystem formerly known as MakerDAO.
- **No Lockups** — Funds remain liquid and can be withdrawn at any time without penalties or waiting periods.
- **Predictable Yield** — The savings rate is set by Sky governance, providing clarity on expected returns rather than fluctuating market rates.
- **Battle-Tested Infrastructure** — Built on the MakerDAO protocol stack, one of the oldest and most audited DeFi systems in the Ethereum ecosystem.
- **High TVL** — Over $2.7B in total value locked, reflecting significant user trust and adoption.
