## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [data.spark.fi/savings](https://data.spark.fi/savings/) and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Acquire USDS** — Obtain USDS stablecoins through the Sky ecosystem or a supported exchange.
3. **Deposit into Savings** — Enter the amount of USDS you wish to deposit and confirm the transaction to start earning the Sky Savings Rate.
4. **Monitor your balance** — Use the Spark Data Hub dashboard to track your accruing yield and savings activity in real time.

## Key Features

- **Sky Savings Rate (SSR)** — Earn a predictable, governance-set APY on your USDS deposits without active management.
- **USDS Stablecoin Support** — Designed specifically for USDS, the native stablecoin of the Sky ecosystem.
- **Real-Time Analytics** — The Spark Data Hub provides live data on savings rates, total deposits, and protocol activity.
- **Non-Custodial** — Users retain control of their assets; deposits and withdrawals are handled via smart contracts on Ethereum.
- **Transparent Rate Setting** — The savings rate is determined by Sky governance, making it auditable and community-driven.
