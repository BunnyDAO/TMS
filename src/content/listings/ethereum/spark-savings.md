## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [data.spark.fi/savings](https://data.spark.fi/savings/) and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Acquire a supported stablecoin** — Ensure you hold a stablecoin that can be converted into USDS, such as DAI or USDC.
3. **Deposit into USDS Savings** — Follow the on-screen prompts to convert your stablecoin to USDS and deposit into the savings contract.
4. **Monitor your yield** — Track your accruing balance and current Sky Savings Rate in real time through the Spark Data Hub dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Sky Savings Rate** — Access a predictable APY on USDS deposits, set by the Sky/MakerDAO governance ecosystem.
- **Stablecoin Deposits** — Deposit commonly used stablecoins and have them seamlessly converted to USDS to begin earning.
- **Real-Time Analytics** — The Spark Data Hub provides live insights into savings rates, total deposits, and protocol activity.
- **MakerDAO Ecosystem Integration** — Built directly on the Sky/MakerDAO infrastructure, benefiting from its established security and governance.
- **Non-Custodial** — Funds are held in audited smart contracts, keeping users in control of their assets at all times.
- **No Protocol Fees** — Accessing the savings rate through Spark Savings carries no additional platform fees beyond standard Ethereum gas costs.
