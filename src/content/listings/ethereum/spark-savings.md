## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [data.spark.fi/savings](https://data.spark.fi/savings/) and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask.
2. **Acquire USDS** — Obtain USDS stablecoins, the native stablecoin of the Sky (formerly Maker) ecosystem, through a DEX or the Sky protocol directly.
3. **Deposit into Spark Savings** — Approve and deposit your USDS to begin earning the Sky Savings Rate automatically.
4. **Monitor your position** — Use the Spark Data Hub dashboard to track your accrued yield and overall savings activity in real time.

## Key Features

- **Sky Savings Rate (SSR)** — Earn a predictable, protocol-set APY on deposited USDS backed by the Maker/Sky ecosystem.
- **Real-time analytics** — The Spark Data Hub provides live insights into savings rates, deposit volumes, and lending activity.
- **Non-custodial** — Users retain control of their funds; deposits are managed entirely by smart contracts on Ethereum.
- **Stablecoin yield** — Avoid price volatility by earning yield on a stablecoin rather than a volatile asset.
- **Maker/Sky ecosystem integration** — Directly benefits from the deep liquidity and established infrastructure of the MakerDAO/Sky protocol.
