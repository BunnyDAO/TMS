## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit aerodrome.finance and connect a compatible wallet (e.g. MetaMask) set to the Base network.
2. **Swap tokens** — Use the Swap interface to exchange any supported tokens on Base with competitive rates and low fees.
3. **Provide liquidity** — Deposit token pairs into liquidity pools to earn trading fees and AERO token emissions.
4. **Lock AERO for veAERO** — Lock your AERO tokens to receive veAERO, which lets you vote on emission gauges and earn a share of protocol fees.

## Key Features

- **veToken Model** — Lock AERO tokens as veAERO to participate in governance, vote on liquidity gauges, and earn protocol revenue.
- **Stable & Volatile Pools** — Supports both correlated asset (stable) and uncorrelated asset (volatile) AMM pool types for efficient trading.
- **Liquidity Incentives** — AERO emissions are directed by veAERO voters each epoch, aligning incentives between liquidity providers and token holders.
- **Low Fees on Base** — Built on Base L2, users benefit from significantly lower gas costs compared to Ethereum mainnet.
- **Gauge Voting** — veAERO holders vote weekly to allocate token emissions to specific liquidity pools, enabling protocols to bootstrap liquidity.
- **Protocol-Owned Ecosystem** — Aerodrome functions as Base's central liquidity layer, with integrations across the Base DeFi ecosystem.
