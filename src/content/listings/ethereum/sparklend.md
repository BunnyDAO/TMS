## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [app.spark.fi](https://app.spark.fi/) and connect a compatible Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect.
2. **Choose an action** — Select whether you want to supply assets to earn yield or borrow stablecoins against your collateral.
3. **Review rates** — Check the current governance-defined interest rates and liquidity conditions before confirming your position.
4. **Manage your position** — Monitor your health factor and adjust collateral or debt as needed to maintain a safe borrow position.

## Key Features

- **Governance-Defined Rates** — Interest rates are set through Sky governance rather than purely by algorithmic supply and demand, providing more predictable borrowing costs.
- **Sky Liquidity Layer** — Consistent USDS, sUSDS, and USDC liquidity is supplied programmatically through Sky's Spark Liquidity Layer, reducing liquidity fragmentation.
- **Stablecoin Focus** — The protocol is purpose-built for stablecoin lending and borrowing, targeting users who want to maximize yield or leverage on stable assets.
- **Transparent Rate Dashboard** — Users can view current and historical rates in a clean interface designed for clarity and informed decision-making.
- **Sky Ecosystem Integration** — Deep integration with the Sky (formerly MakerDAO) ecosystem gives users access to native USDS and savings instruments like sUSDS.
