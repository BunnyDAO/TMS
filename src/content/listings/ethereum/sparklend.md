## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.spark.fi and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby.
2. **Deposit collateral** — Supply supported assets (such as ETH or wstETH) as collateral to enable borrowing.
3. **Borrow stablecoins** — Borrow DAI or other supported stablecoins at governance-defined fixed rates.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and manage collateral to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Fixed Borrow Rates** — Interest rates are set by Sky governance, not utilization curves, providing predictability for borrowers
- **Sky (MakerDAO) Backed** — Protocol is governed and supported by one of the oldest and most established DeFi ecosystems
- **Spark Liquidity Layer** — Deep liquidity infrastructure ensures borrowing capacity even during high demand
- **Stablecoin Focus** — Optimized for borrowing DAI and stablecoins rather than a broad range of volatile assets
- **Transparent Rates** — All rate parameters are publicly visible on-chain and determined through open governance
- **Non-Custodial** — Users retain control of their assets; the protocol operates via audited smart contracts
