## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.spark.fi and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby.
2. **Deposit collateral** — Supply supported crypto assets (e.g., ETH, wstETH) as collateral to the protocol.
3. **Borrow stablecoins** — Borrow USDS or other available stablecoins at the current governance-set fixed rate against your collateral.
4. **Monitor your position** — Keep track of your health factor and collateral ratio to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Governance-Defined Fixed Rates** — Borrowing rates are set by Sky (MakerDAO) governance rather than purely by supply and demand, providing more predictable costs.
- **Spark Liquidity Layer** — A dedicated liquidity infrastructure powers the protocol, enabling deep stablecoin availability.
- **Sky Ecosystem Integration** — Directly integrated with the Sky (MakerDAO) ecosystem, benefiting from its established governance and risk frameworks.
- **Stablecoin Focus** — Optimized for borrowing USDS and other stablecoins, making it a go-to venue for stablecoin liquidity.
- **Earn on Stablecoins** — Users can also supply stablecoins to earn transparent, competitive yields within the protocol.
