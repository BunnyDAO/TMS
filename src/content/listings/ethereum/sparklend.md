## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.spark.fi and connect a Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Supply collateral** — Deposit supported assets (such as ETH or wstETH) to use as collateral.
3. **Borrow stablecoins** — Borrow USDS or other supported stablecoins at governance-set rates.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and manage your collateral ratio to avoid liquidation.

## Key Features

- **Governance-Defined Rates** — Interest rates are set by Sky (MakerDAO) governance rather than fluctuating with utilization, offering more predictable borrowing costs.
- **Spark Liquidity Layer** — A dedicated liquidity infrastructure ensures consistent stablecoin availability across the protocol and broader DeFi ecosystem.
- **USDS Integration** — Native support for USDS, Sky's stablecoin, enabling deep liquidity and tight integration with the Sky ecosystem.
- **Transparent Rate Display** — Rates and protocol parameters are openly published and governed on-chain, allowing users to make informed decisions.
- **Ethereum-Native** — Built on Ethereum, benefiting from its security and broad DeFi composability.
- **Non-Custodial** — Users retain control of their assets at all times through smart contract interactions.
