## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.spark.fi and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby.
2. **Choose an action** — Select whether you want to deposit stablecoins to earn yield or supply collateral to borrow.
3. **Review rates** — Check current governance-defined interest rates before confirming any transaction.
4. **Confirm and monitor** — Approve the transaction in your wallet and track your position from the dashboard.

## Key Features

- **Governance-defined rates** — Interest rates are set by MakerDAO/Sky governance rather than purely by algorithmic supply and demand, providing predictability.
- **USDS stablecoin integration** — Native support for USDS, the stablecoin at the center of the Sky ecosystem, enabling seamless borrowing and earning.
- **Spark Liquidity Layer** — A dedicated liquidity infrastructure layer that ensures consistent stablecoin availability across the protocol.
- **Transparent on-chain rates** — All rates and protocol parameters are publicly visible and governed on-chain.
- **MakerDAO/Sky backing** — Benefits from the security and credibility of one of the most established DeFi ecosystems.
- **Earn on stablecoins** — Deposit idle stablecoins to earn yield without exposure to volatile assets.
