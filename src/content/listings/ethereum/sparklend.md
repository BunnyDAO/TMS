## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit app.spark.fi and connect an Ethereum-compatible wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
2. **Supply assets** — Deposit USDS, USDC, or other supported collateral to start earning yield at governance-defined rates.
3. **Borrow against collateral** — Use your supplied assets as collateral to borrow stablecoins at transparent, predictable rates.
4. **Monitor your position** — Track your health factor and interest accrual directly in the dashboard to manage risk.

## Key Features

- **Governance-Defined Rates** — Interest rates are set through Sky/MakerDAO governance rather than purely algorithmic supply and demand, offering more predictable borrowing costs.
- **Spark Liquidity Layer** — A dedicated liquidity infrastructure that ensures consistent availability of USDS and USDC within the lending market.
- **USDS Integration** — Native support for USDS, the Sky ecosystem stablecoin, enabling deep liquidity and tight integration with MakerDAO's broader infrastructure.
- **Transparent Rate Dashboard** — Users can view current supply and borrow rates clearly, supporting informed decision-making.
- **Sky/MakerDAO Ecosystem** — Built as part of the broader Sky protocol suite, benefiting from established governance, audits, and ecosystem trust.
- **Non-Custodial** — Users retain control of their assets through smart contracts, with no centralized intermediary holding funds.
