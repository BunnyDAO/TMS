## Getting Started

1. **Connect your wallet** — Visit [app.spark.fi](https://app.spark.fi/) and connect a compatible Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby.
2. **Deposit collateral** — Supply accepted collateral assets to the protocol to establish a borrowing position.
3. **Borrow stablecoins** — Borrow USDS or other supported stablecoins at governance-defined rates that do not change based on pool utilization.
4. **Monitor your position** — Keep an eye on your health factor to avoid liquidation and manage your collateral ratio over time.

## Key Features

- **Governance-defined rates** — Interest rates are set by MakerDAO/Sky governance rather than an algorithmic utilization curve, providing more predictable borrowing costs.
- **Spark Liquidity Layer** — A dedicated liquidity infrastructure supplies USDS, sUSDS, and USDC across DeFi, ensuring deep liquidity for borrowers.
- **USDS and sUSDS support** — Native integration with the Sky ecosystem allows users to borrow and earn with USDS and its savings-bearing variant sUSDS.
- **Stablecoin-focused market** — The protocol is purpose-built for stablecoin lending, reducing exposure to volatile asset dynamics common in general-purpose lending markets.
- **MakerDAO integration** — Deep ties to the MakerDAO and Sky protocol provide a trust-minimized backstop and consistent liquidity sourcing.
