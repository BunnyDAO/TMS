## Getting Started

1. **Acquire WBTC** — Purchase WBTC directly on a DEX or CEX such as Uniswap, Coinbase, or Binance without going through the minting process.
2. **Mint via a merchant** — If you hold native BTC and want to mint WBTC, connect with an authorized merchant through the WBTC network to initiate the conversion.
3. **Use in DeFi** — Deposit WBTC into lending protocols, liquidity pools, or yield strategies across Ethereum, Solana, TRON, and other supported networks.
4. **Verify reserves** — Confirm the 1:1 Bitcoin backing at any time using publicly available on-chain proof of reserves tools.

## Key Features

- **1:1 Bitcoin Backing** — Every WBTC token is backed by one Bitcoin held in custody, verified transparently on-chain.
- **Multi-chain Availability** — Native WBTC is accessible on Ethereum, Solana, TRON, and additional networks, broadening DeFi access.
- **Decentralized Governance** — The WBTC DAO oversees protocol decisions in a community-led structure with merchant and custodian participants.
- **DeFi Composability** — As a standard ERC20 token, WBTC integrates seamlessly with the broader Ethereum DeFi ecosystem including lending, trading, and yield platforms.
- **Transparent Reserves** — On-chain proof of reserves allows anyone to audit Bitcoin holdings backing all circulating WBTC at any time.
